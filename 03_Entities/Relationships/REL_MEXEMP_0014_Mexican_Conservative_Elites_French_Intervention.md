# REL_MEXEMP_0014 Mexican conservative elites - French intervention

## Summary / 関係性の大意

メキシコ保守派エリートとFrench interventionの関係は、外国軍事介入に現地政治的顔を与える一方、介入を「保守派内戦の再燃」に見せるリスクも抱えた。Almonte、Miramón、Márquez、Mejíaらはそれぞれ異なる機能を持ち、保守派を単一の親仏集団として扱うと粗くなる。

## Tensions / 緊張・対立・不一致

- フランス側は現地協力者を必要としたが、協力者が保守派色を強めるほど自由派・共和派との和解可能性は下がった。
- Hamnett p.8-9の視点では、Maximilian帝政は外部から押しつけられた制度であるだけでなく、地域主義・personalism・憲法政府未確立という既存の構造問題へ介入した制度として読む必要がある。
- Paredes y Arrillagaのmonarchist viewsは、Maximilian以前の国内君主制志向をたどる補助線にはなるが、本文上の断定度は低いため保守派全体の連続的君主派化として扱わない。
- Hamnett p.16-17の整理では、共和政と君主制の対立はMexico内部の国家形態論争としても扱える。ただしFrench InterventionとSecond Empire成立の外圧性は別に整理する。
- Hamnett p.18-19の整理では、Imperial Franceの関心はTehuantepecのような戦略空間にも向けられる。保守派の内政的動機と、フランス側の地政学的関心を混同しない。
- Miramónの初期拒否は、保守派内部にもMaximilian擁立への懐疑があった材料として読める。ただしMiramón個人の発言を保守派全体へ拡大しない。
- Almonteは有力協力者だが、彼の情報がメキシコ世論全体を代表したわけではない。根拠: [[FACT_MEXEMP_0446]].

## Cooperation / 協力・利害一致

- 保守派はJuárez打倒・教会・秩序回復・反自由派の期待からFrench interventionに接続した。
- フランス側は保守派を通じて、介入を現地政治の要請として見せようとした。
- Regency Councilは、フランス軍支配を隠しながら現地政府形式を作る装置になった。

## Change Over Time / 時期による変化

- 1861-1863: Almonte、Miramón、Márquezらを通じて、保守派とフランス軍の接続が進む。根拠: [[FACT_MEXEMP_0355]], [[FACT_MEXEMP_0357]].
- 1846: Paredes y Arrillagaの独裁未遂と君主制志向の可能性は、Second Empire以前の国内保守・中央集権系政治構想を検討する入口になる。根拠: [[TIME_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0077]], [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0172]].
- 1864: RegencyとJuntaを通じてMaximilian帝政の受け皿が作られる。根拠: [[FACT_MEXEMP_0554]], [[FACT_MEXEMP_0557]].
- 1865: Bazaineの現地行政と保守派協力者の関係が続く。根拠: [[FACT_MEXEMP_0665]], [[FACT_MEXEMP_0666]].
- 1866: French withdrawalで保守派・帝政軍の自立問題が深刻化する。
- 1867: Conservative military actorsがQuerétaro最終局面に残る。

## Interpretive Cautions / 解釈上の注意

- Mexican conservative elitesを、French puppetsとも、純粋な愛国者とも断定しない。
- Paredes y Arrillagaのmonarchist viewsは要確認。Maximilian帝政の直接前史として使う場合も、1846年の独裁未遂・米墨戦争・地域的反発を分けて扱う。
- French Interventionを、欧州外交・メキシコ保守派・国内内戦・米国外交の複合として扱う。
- フランス側資料は現地支持を強調しがちで、共和派・自由派側資料との照合が必要。
- Márquezなど敵対的評判の強い人物は、hostile account、press account、conservative defenseを分ける。

## Novel Use Materials / 小説利用の材料

- フランス軍は保守派を必要とし、保守派はフランス軍を必要としたが、双方の目的が完全一致しない構図を作れる。
- Almonteは政治的窓口、Miramónは懐疑と後の帰還、Márquezは恐怖と戦闘力、Mejíaは地域・先住民保守派という違いを出せる。
- Regency Councilは、独立政府の形を取りながらフランス軍の影が濃い舞台として使える。

## Hamnett 1994 p.22-23 Addendum

- Interpretation / Hamnett p.22-23 places the Church / education conflict in Oaxaca's 1820s Institute controversy, where Liberal Party claims included removal of clerical control of education and secular education. This helps avoid treating 1850s-60s Church / Liberal conflict as sudden or purely empire-era. 根拠: [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0242]], [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0256]], [[CAP_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0014]].
- Caution / Catholic clergy should not be treated as a single reactionary bloc; Fray Francisco Aparicio is cited by Hamnett as a Morelos-supporting cleric. 根拠: [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0246]], [[TIME_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0107]].

## Hamnett 1994 p.24-25 Addendum

- Interpretation / Hamnett p.24-25 links the 1833-34 Gómez Farías reforms to later Reform conflicts over the Church's social position, while also warning that early 1830s political lines were not yet irrevocably drawn. This relationship card should therefore distinguish later Conservative/French Intervention alignments from earlier fluid Church-state politics. 根拠: [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0278]], [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0279]], [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0280]], [[CAP_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0015]].
- Caution / Florencio del Castillo shows that Catholic and Liberal elements could coexist in one figure; do not flatten Church actors into a single anti-Liberal bloc. 根拠: [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0281]], [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0283]].

## Hamnett 1994 p.26-27 Addendum

- Fact / Plan of Cuernavaca defended religion and the corporate prerogatives of Church and army, and León's forces took Oaxaca city on 21 June 1834 under that plan. This is a long prehistory for later Conservative and Church-state alignments, not direct proof of Second Empire support. 根拠: [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0288]], [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0289]], [[TIME_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0131]].
- Interpretation / Hamnett presents the Oaxaca Church-state conflict as superseded by Santa Anna's national intervention and the removal of the Liberal administration. Use this as an example of national intervention overtaking local conflict. 根拠: [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0287]].
- Caution / Castillo's opposition to Gómez Farías, Plan of Cuernavaca reaction, and later French Intervention support are related problems but not identical positions. Church, army, conservative, centralist, and imperial alignments must be separated by date and actor.

## Hamnett 1994 p.28-29 Addendum

- Variant / Hamnett disputes Bulnes's interpretation of León as Santa Anna's Oaxaca proconsul, citing León's earlier federalist and radical Liberal connections. This relationship card should therefore avoid treating centralist or Santa Anna-linked actors as automatically equivalent to later Conservative imperial collaborators. 根拠: [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0324]], [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0325]], [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0326]], [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0327]], [[CAP_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0017]].
- Fact / León refused to cooperate with Canalizo against the Ramírez de Aguilar government in 1833-34, further complicating simple faction labels. 根拠: [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0328]], [[TIME_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0151]].
- Interpretation / Hamnett evaluates León's 1834 and 1841 interventions as mitigating centralism's impact in Oaxaca. Use this as a local-political caution, not as proof of later imperial sympathy or anti-imperial consistency. 根拠: [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0329]].

## Hamnett 1994 p.34-35 Addendum

- Primary testimony / Juárez criticized Paredes-era policy as anti-national and connected state-level conduct to restricted representation. This belongs to the 1840s centralist/federalist conflict and should not be mechanically equated with later Second Empire politics. 根拠: [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0384]], [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0385]], [[CAP_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0020]].
- Interpretation / The polko regime and its removal by Castellanos show Church, military, and local political tensions before the French Intervention. Use as prehistory, not direct proof that all later conservative or imperial actors shared one motive. 根拠: [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0387]], [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0388]].
- Caution / Paredes, polko actors, Mexican Conservatives, and French Intervention collaborators need to remain separated by date, institution, and evidence type.

## Hamnett 1994 p.36-37 Addendum

- Fact / Bernardino Carvajal is described as a cleric, liberal, and personal friend of Juárez. This reinforces that Catholic clergy and Church-linked actors should not be treated as a single anti-Liberal or pro-intervention bloc. 根拠: [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0411]], [[CAP_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0021]].
- Interpretation / The National Guard material shows Liberal state-building required armed force and order maintenance as well as legality. This complicates a simple contrast between conservative coercion and liberal legality. 根拠: [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0423]], [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0425]], [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0427]].
- Caution / Use 1848 Oaxaca politics as institutional prehistory only. Do not collapse local Liberal clerics, later Conservative elites, French officers, and imperial collaborators into a single Church-state axis.

## Hamnett 1994 p.50-51 Addendum

- Interpretation / Hamnett p.50 warns that opposition to Liberal policy could potentially join privileged elites with poorer groups dependent on corporate privilege during the Maximilian period, though he says this did not fully materialize. 根拠: [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0550]], [[CAP_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0028]].
