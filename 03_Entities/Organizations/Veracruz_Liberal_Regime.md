## Hamnett 1994 p.103 Notes

Hamnett p.103 compares state governors' anticlerical initiatives with the Juárez government after it had moved to Veracruz, stressing that several state leaders were ahead of the Veracruz government. The page also begins a Lerdo-Ocampo split over the purpose of disamortization after the government reached Veracruz.

## Hamnett 1994 p.106-107 Notes

Hamnett p.106-107 presents the Veracruz Liberal regime as translating the Manifesto into Reform Laws, using executive authority to separate Church and state, nationalize ecclesiastical property, shift civil registration away from clergy, and prepare enforcement even in enemy-held territory.
