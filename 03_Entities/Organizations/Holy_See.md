# Holy See

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.96 records Munguía's argument that formal permission from the Holy See should be awaited before limiting the ecclesiastical fuero.

## Creative Use

Use it as a jurisdictional reference point in Church-state disputes, not as direct local Church action unless the document confirms it.

## Open Questions

- Confirm whether Munguía cited a specific papal authority or canonical procedure.
