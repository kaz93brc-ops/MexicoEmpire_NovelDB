# Oaxaca Mint

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.78 treats the Oaxaca Mint as an economic institution repeatedly requested from 1824 onward and finally permitted in August 1854, with later importance for Oaxaca mining revival.

## Creative Use

Use the mint as a concrete economic-policy object linking Juárez's state administration to mining, capital scarcity, and local economic regeneration.
