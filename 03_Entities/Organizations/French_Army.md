## Hamnett 1994 pp.174-175 Dependence and Capacity Notes

Hamnett pp.174-175 links Maximilian's sidelining of Conservative generals to deeper French army dependence, while also crediting French forces with considerable capacity under difficult conditions from summer 1863 to spring 1866.
