## Hamnett 1994 p.171 Establishment Notes

Hamnett p.171 opens "THE ESTABLISHMENT OF THE MEXICAN EMPIRE" by stating that Forey transferred sovereignty to a Regency Council including former President Mariano Salas, Conservative politician Juan Nepomuceno Almonte, and Archbishop Labastida; the sentence continues on p.172.

## Hamnett 1994 p.187 Final Regency Notes

Hamnett p.187 records a 20 February 1867 Regency Council appointed by Maximilian from Querétaro, composed of Lares, Lacunza, and Márquez, and interprets the plan as evidence of political self-delusion.
