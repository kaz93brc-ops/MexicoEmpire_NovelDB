# O'Donnell Government

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.169 presents the O'Donnell government as reassuring the [[British_Government|British government]] that [[Spain]] would not join a French plan to interfere in Mexico's political regime or impose [[Maximilian|Archduke Maximilian]].

## Creative Use
