## Hamnett 1994 pp.168-169 Spanish Motive Notes

Hamnett pp.168-169 separates Spain's policy from both British debt enforcement and French imperial design: Serrano and Miramón viewed Spanish connection as a counterweight to U.S. influence, while O'Donnell's government reassured Palmerston's government that Spain would not support French internal intervention plans.
