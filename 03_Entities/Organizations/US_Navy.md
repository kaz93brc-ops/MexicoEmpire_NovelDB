# U.S. Navy

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.152 connects U.S. naval intervention in April 1860 to Juárez's effort to stop Spanish ships contracted by Miramón's regime from closing Veracruz by sea.

## Creative Use

Use as an external force that protects Juárez's Veracruz position while complicating Mexican sovereignty politics.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the ships, commanders, orders, and legal justification for the April 1860 action.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.154-155 Gulf Policy Notes

Hamnett pp.154-155 records the United States reserving naval action in the Gulf to protect U.S. interests while Mexico's lack of a navy left Veracruz vulnerable to European fleets.
