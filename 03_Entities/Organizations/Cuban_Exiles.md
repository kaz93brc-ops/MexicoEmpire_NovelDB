# Cuban Exiles

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.51 connects Juarez's New Orleans exile to meetings among Cuban exiles through Pedro Santacilia; p.52-53 expands the group to Santacilia, Domingo de Goicuría, and Juan Clemente Zenea as figures close to Juarez.

## Creative Use

Use as a network setting for anti-imperial and separatist exile politics.

## Open Questions

- Membership, meeting locations, and connection to Juarez after October 1853.
- Specific roles of Santacilia, Goicuría, and Zenea in Cuban exile politics and Mexican liberal networks.
