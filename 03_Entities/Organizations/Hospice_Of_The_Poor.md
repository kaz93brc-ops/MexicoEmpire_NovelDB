# Hospice of the Poor

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.94-95 presents the Hospice of the Poor as a disputed Oaxaca charitable institution funded by a late-colonial businessman's bequest and tied to Juarez's criticism of Mantecon's investment choices.

## Creative Use

Use as a concrete institution where charity, Church administration, public health, and civil authority can collide.

## Open Questions

- Identify the late-colonial businessman, investment details, legal control, and note 14.
