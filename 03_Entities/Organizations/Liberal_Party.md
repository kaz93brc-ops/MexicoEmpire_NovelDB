## Hamnett 1994 pp.202-203 Convocatoria Split Notes

Hamnett pp.202-203 treats the Convocatoria controversy as a Liberal Party conflict over constitutional reform, popular consultation, state governors' resistance, and Juárez's use of extraordinary powers rather than as a Conservative restoration issue.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.204-205 Party Control Notes

Hamnett pp.204-205 argues that 1867-1872 conflicts strengthened Juárez while revealing his loss of control over the Liberal Party; the party drew support from legislative power and state sovereignty, which Juárez and Lerdo treated as constitutional weaknesses.
