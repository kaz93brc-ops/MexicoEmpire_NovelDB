## Hamnett 1994 p.102-103 Notes

Hamnett p.102-103 emphasizes that after the December 1857 coup, further anticlerical measures came chiefly from state governments and state-level radical actors rather than directly from Juárez. The page names Michoacán, Jalisco, Coahuila, and Zacatecas as important arenas.
