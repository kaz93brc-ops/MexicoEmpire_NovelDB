# Partido Revolucionario Institucional

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.248–249 interprets the PRI order as a twentieth-century continuation and reworking of nineteenth-century Liberal and Juarista political memory.

## Creative Use

Use as an institutional setting in which Juárez symbolism is claimed, adapted, and disputed rather than inherited unchanged.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the exact scope of Hamnett's continuity claim and distinguish old Liberalism, PRI nationalism, and late-twentieth-century neo-liberalism.
