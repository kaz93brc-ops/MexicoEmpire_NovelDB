# Diocese of Puebla

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.97 connects the Puebla diocese to Bishop Labastida's protest and to the government's later decision to charge suppression costs to the diocese.

## Creative Use

Use it to separate Labastida's episcopal protest from the wider local rebellion and government reaction.
