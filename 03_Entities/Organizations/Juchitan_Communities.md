## Hamnett 1994 pp.224-225 Juchitecos Notes

Hamnett pp.224-225 treats Juchitecos as local actors resisting Oaxaca state control and taxation, not as a simple criminal category; the spread records escalation from Albino Jiménez's action to wider Isthmus resistance and Félix Díaz's repression.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.228-229 Félix Díaz Capture Notes

Hamnett pp.228-229 says Juchitán declared loyalty to Juárez while waiting for a chance against El Chato, and later names juchitecos under Albino Jiménez and Benigno Cartas as the captors of Félix Díaz.
