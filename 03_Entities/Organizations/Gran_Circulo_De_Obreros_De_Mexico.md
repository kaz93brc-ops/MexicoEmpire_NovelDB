# Gran Círculo de Obreros de México

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.216 identifies the Gran Círculo de Obreros de México as a worker organization formed in September 1870 under anarchist influence, with branches in Toluca and San Luis Potosí the following year.

## Creative Use

Use as a worker-organization setting where radical ideas, Liberal political openness, and pro-administration infiltration intersect.
