# Department Governors

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.93-95 refers to department governors as state administrative officials who received Juarez's 24 January 1849 instruction and later appeared in the request for church-property information.

## Creative Use

Use as the local administrative layer through which Juarez's state policy reached districts, clergy, and villages.
