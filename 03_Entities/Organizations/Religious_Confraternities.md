# religious confraternities

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.20-21では、18世紀の一部共同体がdistrict administratorsから共同地を守るために土地を移した先として言及される。

## Creative Use

## Hamnett 1994 p.93 Notes

Hamnett p.93 uses confraternities to distinguish village resistance to parish dues from opposition to religion itself: village income could support religious practice even while villagers resisted supporting parish clergy.
