# Chihuahua State Congress

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.128 says the State Congress of Chihuahua condemned the anti-Juárez maneuver on 1861-10-21 as an attempt to subvert constitutional order and the democratic system.

## Creative Use

Use to show that Juárez's 1861 support included provincial institutional voices, not only Mexico City allies.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the original text, vote, and legal form of the 1861-10-21 resolution.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.136-137 Juarez-Terrazas Conflict Notes

Hamnett p.137 says Chihuahua's local Congress refused to publish Juárez's 6 April 1864 state-of-siege decree and asked him to desist; the same spread links Terrazas's legislative support to land grants.
