# Liberal "reds"

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.103 uses Liberal "reds" for a radical Liberal current that attacked traditional clerical roles and Catholic doctrine, held influence in the Liberal Army, and remained cool toward Juárez.

## Creative Use

Use to keep radical Liberal anticlerical currents separate from Juárez's government and from Liberalism as a whole.
