# Permanent Deputation

## Historical Role

In Hamnett's pp.140-141 account, the Permanent Deputation summoned Congress in 1865 and issued a 27 November 1865 manifesto explaining the original intention of that summons.

## Creative Use

Use as an institutional voice for republican continuity when a full Congress could not reach quorum.
