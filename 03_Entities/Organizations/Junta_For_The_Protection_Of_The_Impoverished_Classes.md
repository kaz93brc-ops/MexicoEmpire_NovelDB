# Junta For The Protection Of The Impoverished Classes

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.214 describes this Maximilian-era body as active in 1865-1867 and as a protection mechanism for poor or rural claimants against land loss and infringements on customary rights.

## Creative Use

Use as a remembered imperial-era petition channel that complicates simple Liberal versus Imperial alignment in village land politics.
