# Zacatecas Division

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.126-127 identifies the Zacatecas Division as the Liberal military force associated with González Ortega that occupied Mexico City for several months after the Liberal victory.

## Creative Use

Use as the visible military presence that makes González Ortega's post-victory claim feel coercive inside the capital.
