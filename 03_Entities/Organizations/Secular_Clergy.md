# Secular clergy

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.87 contrasts the secular clergy with the Dominicans / Order of Preachers in Oaxaca's eighteenth-century church structure.

## Creative Use

Use this to distinguish religious orders from parish or diocesan clergy in Oaxaca scenes.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the local balance between secular clergy and regular orders in Oaxaca after the mid-eighteenth century.

## Hamnett 1994 p.93 Notes

Hamnett p.93 places secular clergy at the center of Juárez-Mantecón cooperation and village dues conflict: both leaders worried about rural clergy abuses, while parish priests complained that dues were unpaid.
