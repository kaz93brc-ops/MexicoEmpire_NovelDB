# Diocese of Oaxaca

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.87 says that in 1910 Spanish speakers were only 51 percent of the Diocese of Oaxaca's population, a statistic he uses to frame hybrid Catholicism and village religious institutions.

## Creative Use

Use this as a regional church context distinct from Mexico City and Puebla.

## Hamnett 1994 p.91 Notes

Hamnett p.91 states that the Diocese of Oaxaca had lacked a bishop since 1827 and was not included in Gregory XVI's 1831 appointments of six Mexican bishops.

Hamnett pp.94-95 places the Diocese of Oaxaca in public works, parish dues, hospital repair, the Hospice of the Poor, Church wealth, and statistical inquiry; this shows practical cooperation and conflict with Juarez's state government.
