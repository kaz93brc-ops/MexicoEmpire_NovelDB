# Buchanan Administration

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.149 treats the Buchanan administration as weighing Juárez recognition in relation to loans, territorial concessions, and transit rights during the Reform War.

## Creative Use

Use as a U.S. policy arena rather than a single voice; party politics, southern expansionism, and diplomatic bargaining all overlap here.
