# Oaxaca Cathedral Clergy

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.100 distinguishes the cathedral clergy or canons from Bishop Domínguez in the failed third Te Deum episode. They refused cooperation and kept the cathedral doors closed, with opposition partly tied to the Ley Juárez.

## Creative Use

Use this group to avoid making "the Church" a single actor: local cathedral clergy can apply pressure even when the bishop has previously compromised.
