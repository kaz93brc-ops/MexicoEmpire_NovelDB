# Council of State

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.173 notes a newly created and appointed Council of State in the Second Mexican Empire, with Teodosio Lares joining it on 1864-12-04.

## Creative Use

Use as an institutional marker of Maximilian's attempt to organize imperial rule through appointed advisory structures.
