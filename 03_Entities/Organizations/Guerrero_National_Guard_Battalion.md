# Guerrero National Guard Battalion

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.224 says Félix Díaz sent the Guerrero National Guard Battalion against Juchitán, but the deployment failed to suppress the Juchitecos.

## Creative Use

Use as a state military unit showing how National Guard institutions could become instruments of Oaxaca state coercion after 1867.
