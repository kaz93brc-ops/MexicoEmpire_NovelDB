# Finance Ministry

## Historical Role

In Hamnett p.65, Miguel Lerdo served as finance minister from May 1856 to January 1857 and issued the Lerdo Law with congressional support.

## Creative Use

Use as the institutional setting for fiscal and property reform in the Comonfort government.
