# Mexico City Conservative Regime

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.52 describes a Conservative regime in Mexico City that sought naval aid from Cuba and a blockade of Liberal ports in 1859.

## Creative Use

Use as the institutional counterweight to the Veracruz Liberal regime during the Reform War.

## Hamnett 1994 p.124-125 Notes

Hamnett pp.124-125 uses the Conservative regime as the recognized outcome of the December 1857 coup in Juárez's objection to European mediation, and as the regime the Liberal Party ultimately overthrew after US recognition strengthened Juárez.
