# La Victoria

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.175 cites a 28 January 1864 editorial in La Victoria as framing Mexican resistance against Napoleon III through European liberal and anti-monarchical politics.

## Creative Use

Use as an external press voice that reads Juárez, Lincoln, Garibaldi, and the Mexican republicans inside a wider liberal struggle.
