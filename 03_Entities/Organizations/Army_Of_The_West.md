# Army of the West

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.186-187 associates Corona's Army of the West with the entry into Guadalajara and the convergence on Querétaro in early 1867.

## Creative Use

Use as an entry point for the western republican military pressure that helped close the imperial field around Querétaro.

## Open Questions

- Confirm formal Spanish name, command structure, and operational route in early 1867.
- Confirm relationship with Corona's forces at Guadalajara and Querétaro.
