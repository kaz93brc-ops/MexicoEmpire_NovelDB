# Sanchez Navarro Family

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.176-177 presents the Sánchez Navarro family as a Coahuila landowning family with Conservative ties and imperial collaboration that made it a target of Juárez-side confiscation.

## Creative Use

Use the family to show how wartime allegiance, court connection, and landholding could converge without making all large landowners automatic republican enemies.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the family's property accumulation from 1765 and the scope of the latifundio.
- Confirm the family's Conservative role, relationship with Vidaurri, and imperial-court connections.
- Confirm the legal basis and execution of confiscation in 1865-1866.
