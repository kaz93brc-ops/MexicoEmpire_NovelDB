# Franklin, New Mexico

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.157 says Juárez used the Mexican consul in Franklin, New Mexico, to send official correspondence to Matías Romero from late 1864 onward.

## Creative Use

Use as a narrow communications relay for the mobile republican government during the Chihuahua exile.
