# Chalco

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.214-215 places Chalco at the center of hacienda pressure, railway-linked land-value pressure, Julio Chávez López's rebellion, and Mariano Riva Palacio's 1870 property sale.

## Creative Use

Use as a central-Mexican agrarian setting where railway expansion, hacienda pressure, village land claims, and Liberal state coercion intersect.
