# Nayarit

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.216-217 uses Nayarit and the Tepic district as a case of agrarian and regional resistance shaped by community rights, Guadalajara commercial pressure, Lozada's power, and Juárez's post-1867 compromise.

## Creative Use

Use as a regional setting where community survival, trade control, state power, and shifting national alignments are inseparable.

## Open Questions

- Confirm its administrative status in this period as part of Jalisco's Pacific seaboard / Tepic district.
- Confirm the formal transition to military district status after July 1867.
