# Hacienda of La Noria

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.225 presents the Hacienda of La Noria near Oaxaca City as property granted to Porfirio Díaz for his anti-Intervention service and as the center of political plotting before the 1871 La Noria rebellion.

## Creative Use

Use as the physical setting where Porfirio Díaz's military prestige becomes a postwar anti-Juárez political resource.
