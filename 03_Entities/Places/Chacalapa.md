# Chacalapa

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.230-231 identifies Chacalapa as the place where Félix Díaz / El Chato Díaz was killed after capture by juchitecos in the final Oaxaca phase of the La Noria rebellion.

## Creative Use

Use as the location where Félix Díaz's flight becomes political death and local-revenge memory, without sensationalizing the body violence.

## Open Questions

- Confirm exact location, administrative district, and relation to Pochutla, Juchitán, and the execution route.
- Verify the killing date, sequence, and named actors through note 13 and primary sources.
