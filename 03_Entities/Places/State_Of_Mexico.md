# State Of Mexico

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.214-215 connects the State of Mexico to Chalco-area agrarian conflict and to Governor Mariano Riva Palacio's 1870 sale of Chalco property.

## Creative Use

Use as a state-level frame for central Mexican land pressure and Liberal officeholding tied to property interests.
