# Oaxaca Cathedral

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.100-101 uses Oaxaca Cathedral as the site of Te Deum ceremonies for Juárez's return and constitutional oath, and as the place whose closed doors marked the failed third Te Deum for Juárez's constitutional governorship.

## Creative Use

The cathedral can function as a visible threshold between public religious ceremony and civil republican ceremony.

## Open Questions

- Confirm cathedral records for the 1856-01-10, 1857-03-23, and 1857-06-27 events.
- Confirm the role of cathedral canons and clergy in closing the doors.
