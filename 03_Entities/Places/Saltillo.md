# Saltillo

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.53 names Saltillo as part of the route to which Manuel Quesada escorted Juárez and the moving government in 1863.

## Creative Use

Use as a northern waypoint in scenes of the republican government in movement.

## Hamnett 1994 p.134 Notes

Hamnett p.134 places Juárez's early-1864 refuge in Saltillo inside Vidaurri's domain and makes Saltillo the setting for the 9 January letter delivered by representatives of Doblado and González Ortega.
