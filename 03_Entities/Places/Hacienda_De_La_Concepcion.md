# Hacienda de La Concepción

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.67 identifies Hacienda de La Concepción as the basis of the Esperón family's wealth and as a source of rural tension that continued up to the 1910 Revolution.

## Creative Use

Use as a local setting where Liberal property law, family wealth, municipal authority, and Indigenous community land claims intersect.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the hacienda's location, ownership chain, leased lands, and the concrete form of the tensions that persisted to 1910.
