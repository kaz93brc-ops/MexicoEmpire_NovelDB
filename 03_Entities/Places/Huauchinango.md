# Huauchinango

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.208-211 places Rafael Cravioto at Huauchinango and connects the district to Puebla sierra politics, land-law enforcement, and the struggle over Zacapoaxtla and Zacatlán.

## Creative Use

Use as a Puebla highland base where local officials, sierra chiefs, and the Juárez government monitor each other's movements.

## Open Questions

- Confirm Cravioto's office at Huauchinango and the district's relation to the 1863 land-law enforcement problem.
