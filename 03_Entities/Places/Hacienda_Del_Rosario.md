# Hacienda del Rosario

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.76 says José María Díaz Ordaz purchased Hacienda del Rosario, one of the principal estates in the Valley of Oaxaca, under the Lerdo Law.

## Creative Use

Use as a material sign that Liberal Reform could create or reinforce regional landed power, not only dismantle older corporate property.
