# Xochiapulco

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.210-211 identifies Xochiapulco as Lucas's base for uprisings in mid-November and again on 21 November 1871.

## Creative Use

Use as a local highland setting where Puebla's unresolved factional and military conflicts reappear across several years.

## Open Questions

- Confirm Lucas's position at Xochiapulco, the year of the mid-November uprising, and the details of the 21 November 1871 rising.
