# San Mateo Xindihui

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.229 identifies San Mateo Xindihui, near Nochixtlán in the Mixteca Alta, as the site where Alatorre's advance defeated the Díaz-side commander Mier y Terán on 22 December 1871.

## Creative Use

Use as the military turning-point location that opened Alatorre's route toward Oaxaca City during La Noria.

## Open Questions

- Confirm spelling, location, relation to Nochixtlán, and battle details.
- Confirm whether the defeated commander should be linked definitively to Luis Mier y Terán.
