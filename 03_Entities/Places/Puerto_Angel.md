# Puerto Ángel

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.229 places Puerto Ángel in Félix Díaz's early January 1872 escape route as the port where he missed the ship that might have carried him away.

## Creative Use

Use as the coastal escape point where Félix Díaz's flight from Oaxaca fails and turns into concealment near Pochutla.
