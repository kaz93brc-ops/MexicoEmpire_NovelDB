# Zimatlan

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.94 describes Zimatlan as a Zapotec town involved in a parish-dues dispute in September 1849 and notes that it had lost land to adjacent private estates in the eighteenth century.

## Creative Use

Use as a village setting where parish dues, land loss, Zapotec community interests, episcopal pressure, and Juarez's legal-order politics intersect.
