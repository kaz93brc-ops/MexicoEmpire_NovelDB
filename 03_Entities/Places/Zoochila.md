# Zoochila

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.101 describes Zoochila as a sierra mining town whose parish priest was ordered arrested and sent to the federal capital in late June 1857 after refusing last rites and Christian burial over the constitutional oath.

## Creative Use

Zoochila can stage the local consequences of constitutional oath policy, sacramental discipline, and state public-order enforcement.
