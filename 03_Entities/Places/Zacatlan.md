# Zacatlán

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.210 reports Cravioto urging Juárez to secure Zacatlán together with Zacapoaxtla to prevent further deterioration in Puebla and Veracruz.

## Creative Use

Use as one of the mountain towns whose control signals whether the restored republic can reach the Puebla sierra.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the military status of Zacatlán in early 1869 and its relation to Zacapoaxtla and Veracruz routes.
