# Zacapoaxtla

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.97 identifies Zacapoaxtla in the northern Puebla sierra as the base of a rebellion linked to the Puebla crisis after the Juárez Law.

## Creative Use

Use it as a mountain rebel base connecting religious defense, centralist constitutional memory, and anti-Comonfort politics.

## Open Questions

- Identify the parish priest, routes toward Puebla city, and local support base.
