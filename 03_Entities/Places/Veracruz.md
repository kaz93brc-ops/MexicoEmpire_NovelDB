# Veracruz

## Overview

EuropeおよびUSA東海岸との主要接触線となる港湾。Puebla商業社会と結びつき、1850年代半ばにはLiberalism支持の中心とされる。

## Hamnett 1994 Notes

- Puebla商業社会の重要な港湾接続先。

## Hamnett 1994 pp.154-155 Naval Vulnerability Notes

Hamnett pp.154-155 presents Veracruz as exposed to European fleets because Mexico lacked a navy, while the United States reserved possible naval action in the Gulf for U.S. interests.

## Hamnett 1994 p.121 Veracruz Regime Notes

Hamnett p.121 makes Veracruz the wartime seat of the Liberal government after Juárez's 1858-05-04 arrival via Panama, but also stresses that Veracruz politics included Gutiérrez Zamora, the red wing, Miguel Lerdo, and cabinet rivalry.
