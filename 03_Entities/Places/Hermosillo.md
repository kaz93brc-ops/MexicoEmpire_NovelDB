# Hermosillo

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.185 notes Hermosillo as the Sonora location taken by juarista forces on 1866-08-14 during the northern republican advance.

## Creative Use

Use as a northern-theater marker for the military pressure that accompanied French withdrawal and the collapse of imperial defense.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the military context and commanders involved in the 1866-08-14 capture.
- Confirm whether later Hamnett pages expand the Sonora campaign.
