# Ocotlán

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.93 mentions Ocotlán as one of the places where private donations were available for primary education during Juárez's Oaxaca governorship.

## Creative Use

Use as a local setting for school-funding negotiations that involved Juárez, the bishop, and parish resources.
