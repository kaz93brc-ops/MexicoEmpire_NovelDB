# Ameca

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.230-231 places Porfirio Díaz at Ameca, Jalisco, on 3 April 1872, where he modified the Plan of La Noria in an attempt to attract lerdista support.

## Creative Use

Use as the location of Díaz's tactical revision of the rebellion's political formula after earlier support failed to materialize.
