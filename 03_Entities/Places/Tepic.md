# Tepic

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.66 uses the Tepic district as a rural resistance setting where Manuel Lozada led opposition and defied the central government into the early 1870s.

## Creative Use

Use as a regional counterpoint to Mexico City and Oaxaca, showing how Liberal Reform met local armed resistance.

## Open Questions

- Confirm administrative status, Lozada's territorial base, and connections to Reform War, French Intervention, and later republican campaigns.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.218-219 Notes
