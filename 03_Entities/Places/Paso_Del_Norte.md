# Paso del Norte

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.135 identifies Paso del Norte, now Ciudad Juarez, as Juarez's residence during two periods in 1865-1866.

## Creative Use

Use as the border setting for the mobile republican government at the edge of exile and continued resistance.
