# Durango

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.134-137 connects Durango to Patoni's governorship and to Juárez's later appointment of Patoni as military commander over Durango and Chihuahua.

## Creative Use

Use as a northern state power base linked to Patoni's role in Juárez's attempt to remove Terrazas.
