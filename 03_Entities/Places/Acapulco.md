# Acapulco

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.57 places Comonfort's revised Plan of Ayutla at Acapulco on 1854-03-11.

## Creative Use

Use as the coastal setting where the Ayutla program gained an explicitly Liberal coloring.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.212-213 Guerrero Notes

Hamnett pp.212-213 makes Acapulco a Guerrero civil-war fiscal and military point: Arce stationed troops there, Álvarez saw that as threatening customs-revenue control, and the federal government later allowed Acapulco customs revenue to pay Álvarez's coastal battalions.
