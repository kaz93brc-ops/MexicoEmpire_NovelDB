# Lower California

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.105 states that the United States government showed no interest in a loan secured by Mexican Church property sales and instead sought the cession of Lower California.

## Creative Use

Use as a territorial-sovereignty pressure point in Lerdo's failed United States loan negotiations.
