# Federal District

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.194-195 states that Porfirio Díaz appointed Juan José Baz as jefe político of the Federal District around the fall of Mexico City.

## Creative Use

Use as the administrative space for republican power reorganization after the capital's fall.
