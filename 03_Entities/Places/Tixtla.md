# Tixtla

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.212-213 places Tixtla inside the Guerrero civil-war setting, where National Guard control, the state government, Arce's position, and Jiménez factional strength became linked.

## Creative Use

Use as a Guerrero political center where local armed control and state authority intersect after the Restoration.
