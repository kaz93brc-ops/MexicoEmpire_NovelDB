# Puebla

## Overview

中央渓谷、南部砂糖地帯、北部高地、繊維産業、保護主義、Puebla National Guardなど複数の地域・経済・政治要素を持つ州。

## Hamnett 1994 Notes

- 中央渓谷、南部砂糖地帯、北部高地で異なる人口・経済・社会的軌跡をたどった。

## Hamnett 1994 pp.154-155 Battle of Puebla Notes

Hamnett pp.154-155 treats Puebla's 5 May 1862 victory as a military delay that gave the Juárez government time to try arms purchases in the United States.

## Hamnett 1994 p.87 Chapter 5 Notes

Hamnett p.87 identifies Puebla, alongside Mexico City, as a major center of the Catholic Church's institutional strength in colonial Mexico, and contrasts this with Oaxaca's smaller ecclesiastical establishment.
