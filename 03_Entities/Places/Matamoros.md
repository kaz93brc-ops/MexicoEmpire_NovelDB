## Hamnett 1994 p.135 Border Commerce Notes

Hamnett p.135 identifies Matamoros as central to Southern cotton exports after the Union blockade of Texas ports and to the profitable 1862-64 border commerce tied to Vidaurri and Monterrey interests.
