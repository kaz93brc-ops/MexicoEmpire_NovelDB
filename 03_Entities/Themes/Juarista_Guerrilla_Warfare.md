## Hamnett 1994 p.181 Regional Guerrilla Notes

Hamnett p.181 states that Juárez believed strongly in guerrilla warfare and then gives Veracruz, Huasteca, and Guerrero examples, while also noting the French counter-guerrilla response under Colonel Dupin.
