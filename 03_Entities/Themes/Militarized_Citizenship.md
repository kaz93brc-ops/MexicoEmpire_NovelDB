# Militarized Citizenship

## Definition

Theme for the liberal idea that citizens could owe militia or National Guard service alongside legal and representative rights.

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.49 links republican citizenship with National Guard service after 1846. This extends earlier Oaxaca National Guard material from Chapter 2.

## Creative Use

Use to prevent republicanism from becoming only the language of freedom; it also asks bodies, taxes, and arms from citizens.
