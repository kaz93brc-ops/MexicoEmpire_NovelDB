# Fuero eclesiástico

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.96-97 treats the ecclesiastical fuero as the jurisdictional privilege contested by the Juárez Law and defended by Munguía as more than a mere privilege.

## Creative Use

Use it as a jurisdictional conflict over courts and sovereignty, not as a generic symbol of faith.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the legal scope before and after the Juárez Law and compare Munguía's claim with the statutory text.
