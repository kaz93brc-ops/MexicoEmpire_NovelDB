# Mexicanizing The War

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.179 uses "Mexicanizing the war" to describe the hard reality of Napoleon III's policy as French forces withdrew and imperial Mexican forces faced republican armies.

## Creative Use

Use as a political-military frame for the Empire being left to fight with weaker local resources after French support contracted.
