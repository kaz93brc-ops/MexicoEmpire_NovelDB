# Constitutionalism Of 1857

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.55 presents the progressive wing through Guzmán as insisting on strict execution of the 1857 Constitution.

## Creative Use

Use as the legal-purity pole against which Juárez's wartime pragmatism can be contrasted.
