# Jefe Político

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.74 identifies the jefe político as a local administrative-political authority whose control of electoral processes could shape election results under the 1857 constitutional order.

Hamnett p.73 also places jefes políticos inside state-level centralism: state governors appointed them, and they touched municipal control, disputed elections, army recruitment, and tierras baldías.

## Creative Use

Use this institution to show how liberal electoral ideals passed through local administrative power instead of appearing as a direct popular voice.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the legal powers, regional variation, and concrete election-management practices of jefes políticos in the 1850s.
- Confirm the institutional history, number of officials, and Díaz-period notoriety noted by Hamnett.
