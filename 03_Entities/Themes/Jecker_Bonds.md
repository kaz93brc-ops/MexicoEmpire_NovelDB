# Jecker Bonds

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.168 treats the Jecker bonds as a Miramón-era debt arrangement whose inflated claim became one path by which private finance entered French diplomatic pressure against Mexico.

## Creative Use

Use as a concrete financial claim that can connect conservative wartime borrowing, banking interests, and French imperial policy.
