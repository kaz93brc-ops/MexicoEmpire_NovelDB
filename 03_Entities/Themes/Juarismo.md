# Juarismo

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.129 uses juarismo as a way to describe official Liberalism around Juárez in relation to provincial Liberal cadres during the 1861-63 crisis.

## Creative Use

Use to distinguish Juárez-centered official Liberalism from broader Liberal factions, state governors, and congressional opposition.

## Open Questions

- Confirm whether Hamnett is using the term analytically, as contemporary language, or as later historiographical shorthand.
- Distinguish juarismo from the Liberal Party as an organization.

## Hamnett 1994 p.180 Northern Character Notes

Hamnett p.180 adds that juarismo, though rooted in Oaxaca, acquired a northern character through Juárez's 1865-66 survival strategy and northern rear base, with afterlives into anti-central political language after Juárez's death.
