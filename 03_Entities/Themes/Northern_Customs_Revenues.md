# Northern Customs Revenues

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.137 treats northern frontier customs revenues as a fiscal resource that the hard-pressed federal government could lose to state or regional control, and that returned during Trías's governorship.

## Creative Use

Use to connect border finance, regional power, and Juárez's wartime federal authority problem.
