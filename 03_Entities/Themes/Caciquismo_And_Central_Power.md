# Caciquismo And Central Power

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.57 interprets the Plan of Ayutla as emerging from traditional local cacique hostility toward central power before it became an explicitly Liberal statement.

## Creative Use

Use to keep local power politics visible inside Liberal Reform origins.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.202-203 Restored Republic Notes

Hamnett pp.202-203 returns to caciquismo in the late Juárez period by arguing that Juárez rejected caudillos, caciques, and factions in principle but used them in practice when necessary, so constitutionalism and caciquismo should be studied through their practical interaction.
