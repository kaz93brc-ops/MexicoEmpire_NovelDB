# State Radicalism

## Historical Role

Theme for Hamnett's argument that state-level radical Liberals and governor-linked forces could use weak central power to push reform, dispose of Church property, and control fiscal resources.

## Creative Use

Use to show that provincial actors were not merely auxiliaries of Mexico City but competitors for leadership of reform.

## Open Questions

- Confirm which states and governors Hamnett has in mind and whether the Church property and federal revenue claims are Hamnett's own interpretation or drawn from Bulnes or another source.
