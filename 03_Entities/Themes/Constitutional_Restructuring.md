## Hamnett 1994 pp.202-203 Convocatoria Notes

Hamnett pp.202-203 frames the 14 August 1867 Convocatoria as Juárez and Lerdo's attempt to submit five constitutional reforms to a broad electorate, with bicameralism as the principal proposal and congressional predominance as the problem to be corrected.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.232-233 Constitutional Reform Revived Notes

Hamnett pp.232-233 shows Juárez reviving constitutional reform on 1872-04-01, chiefly through the Senate proposal, and frames the death-section opening around the additional reform time Juárez might have had if he had completed the 1871 term.
