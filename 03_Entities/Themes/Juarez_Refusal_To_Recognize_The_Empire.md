# Juarez Refusal To Recognize The Empire

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.178-179 treats Juárez's refusal to compromise with Maximilian as a refusal to concede foreign authority over Mexico's political form, not merely a personal rivalry.

## Creative Use

Use as a legitimacy frame for scenes where Juárez refuses imperial negotiation while preserving the distinction between sovereignty claims and wartime coercion.
