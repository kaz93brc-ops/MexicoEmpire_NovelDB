# Juárez's Northern Rear Base

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.180 frames Sonora, Sinaloa, Durango, and Chihuahua as a northern rear base for juarismo during 1865-66.

## Creative Use

Use to keep Juárez's survival strategy grounded in regional support rather than isolated flight.
