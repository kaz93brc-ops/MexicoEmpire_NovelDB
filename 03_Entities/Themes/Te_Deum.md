# Te Deum

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.100-101 uses Te Deum ceremonies to trace the movement from Church-state cooperation in Oaxaca to rupture over Juárez's constitutional governorship.

## Creative Use

The ritual can mark when public legitimacy still depends on cathedral ceremony and when Juárez shifts to civil ceremony.
