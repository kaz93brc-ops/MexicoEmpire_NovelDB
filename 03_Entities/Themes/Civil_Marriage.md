# Civil Marriage

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.103 treats civil marriage as part of González Ortega's state-level anticlerical legislation in Zacatecas.

Hamnett p.108 treats the 1859-07-23 civil marriage law as a Veracruz Reform measure that made marriage a civil contract before state authority, while leaving religious blessing to each confession's rules.

## Creative Use

Use to stage Church-state conflict through family law rather than only through property disputes.

## Open Questions

- Confirm date, decree name, legal scope, and relationship to federal Reform Laws.
- Confirm the 1859 civil marriage law's formal title and its separation/divorce provisions.
