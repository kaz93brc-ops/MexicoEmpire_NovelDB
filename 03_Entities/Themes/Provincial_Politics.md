## Hamnett 1994 pp.200-201 Notes

Hamnett pp.200-201 argues that state politics often determined national outcomes in the late Juárez period and uses Guerrero and Puebla highland examples to show the limits of national authority.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.208-209 Puebla Notes

Hamnett pp.208-209 develops the same theme through Puebla, Zacatecas, and San Luis Potosí: provincial factional struggles became national political opposition, while Puebla's sierra conflict mixed local legitimacy, military resources, Church-property enforcement, and Congressional pressure.
