# Federal Republic And Representation

## Definition

Theme for the liberal goal of a popular, representative, federal republic in post-colonial Mexico.

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.49 uses this formulation to summarize the aims of three generations of liberals and to place Juárez's 1847 gubernatorial language within that tradition.
