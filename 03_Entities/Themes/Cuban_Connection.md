# Cuban Connection

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.51 says Juarez's Cuban connection began in October 1853; p.52-53 connects it to Cuban exiles, support for Mexican Liberals, Juarez family networks, and later Cuban revolutionary politics.

## Creative Use

Use as a bridge between New Orleans exile scenes, Juarez family support, and broader anti-Spanish imperial and liberal international networks.
