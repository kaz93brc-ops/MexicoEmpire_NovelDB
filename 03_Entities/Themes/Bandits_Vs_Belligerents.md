# Bandits Vs Belligerents

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.182-184 presents the bandits-versus-belligerents distinction as the legal-political conflict behind anti-guerrilla violence and prisoner exchange.

## Creative Use

Use to show that prisoner exchange was also a recognition dispute over whether republicans were criminal bands or legitimate belligerents.
