# Constitutional Oath

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.99 treats the government's requirement that public office-holders swear to uphold the 1857 Constitution as a serious political error that sharpened Church-state conflict.

Hamnett p.100 completes the archbishop's policy by making absolution conditional on public retraction of the oath and notice to civil authorities. Hamnett p.101 then shows the same oath problem reaching last rites and Christian burial in the Zoochila/Tavehua incident.

## Creative Use

Use this theme where political legality and sacramental life collide, especially in confession scenes or public-office dilemmas.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the legal form of the oath requirement and which public office-holders were covered.
- Confirm the archbishop's identity and note 22 for the absolution policy.
- Confirm the Zoochila/Tavehua case, note 25, and the local offices involved.
