# Constitutional Workability

## Historical Role

Theme for disputes over whether the 1857 Constitution could actually govern Mexico, especially under weak executive power and strong state governments.

## Creative Use

Use to frame Liberal disagreements without reducing them to loyalty or betrayal.

## Open Questions

- Confirm Silíceo's source, Knapp's argument, and Hamnett's line between historical claim and author interpretation.
- Track how the workability problem extends into the Restored Republic and Porfirian justifications.
