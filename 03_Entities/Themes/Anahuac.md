# Anáhuac

## Historical Role

In Hamnett pp.194-195, Anáhuac appears as a national-historical term in Juárez's Manifesto justifying the execution of Maximilian, tied to Mexico, Aztec memory, and anti-imperial republican sovereignty.

## Creative Use

Use as a public nationalist and symbolic vocabulary, not as direct proof of Juárez's private ethnic identity or simple Aztec policy continuity.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the Manifesto's original wording, date, and the exact context of Anáhuac, Aztec, conquest, and Charles V references.
