# Electoral College

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.74 identifies electoral colleges as the bodies that selected deputies and the president under the indirect electoral structure of the 1857 Constitution.

## Creative Use

Use electoral colleges as a middle layer between popular suffrage and national office-holding.
