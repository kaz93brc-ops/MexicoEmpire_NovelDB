# Juarista Priests

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.112 uses "Juárista priests" for a small group of clergy aligned with Juárez or the Liberal cause and compares them analytically with insurgent priests from the independence era.

## Creative Use

Use this as a pressure point between lower clergy, Catholic hierarchy, Liberal national-church hopes, and local religious life, without treating it as a formal organization.
