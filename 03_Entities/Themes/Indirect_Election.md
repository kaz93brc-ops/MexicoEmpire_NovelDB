# Indirect Election

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.74 describes the 1857 Constitution as using indirect elections in which voters selected electors and electoral colleges selected deputies and the president.

## Creative Use

Use indirect election to complicate scenes that might otherwise present constitutional voting as simple mass democracy.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the constitutional provisions, election laws, and continuity from 1791 and 1812 models.

## Hamnett 1994 p.77 Notes

Hamnett p.77 applies the indirect-election problem specifically to Oaxaca, where indirect voting coexisted with the 1857 Constitution's adult male suffrage principle and with a state election law based on population representation.
