# Republican Citizenship

## Definition

Theme for citizenship in a liberal republic, including rights before the law, representation, taxation, militia service, and loyalty to the nation.

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.49 frames liberal citizenship as a package of rights and obligations. It should not be reduced to abstract liberty alone.
