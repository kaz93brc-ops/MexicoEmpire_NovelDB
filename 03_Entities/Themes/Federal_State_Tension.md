# Federal State Tension

## Definition

連邦政府の行政措置、州政府の合法性、州財政負担、連邦議員経費をめぐる中央・州関係。

## Hamnett 1994 pp.202-203 Convocatoria Governor Conflict Notes

Hamnett pp.202-203 extends federal-state tension into the Restored Republic: Puebla governor Juan N. Méndez and Guanajuato governor Florencio Antillón refused to publish the Convocatoria, Juárez removed them through extraordinary powers, and their replacements changed jefes políticos before elections.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.224-225 Oaxaca and La Noria Notes

Hamnett pp.224-225 extends federal-state tension to Oaxaca: a state-based National Guard became an anti-Juárez resource, all Oaxaca districts reportedly backed Porfirio Díaz in 1871, and the Oaxaca state government published the Plan of La Noria while initially suspending federal recognition.
