# Nation Versus Community

## Definition

Theme for tensions between loyalty to the nation and attachments to village, community, family, patronage, corporate body, or privileged estate.

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.49 argues that liberal republican citizenship expected national belonging to outrank local and corporate identities.
