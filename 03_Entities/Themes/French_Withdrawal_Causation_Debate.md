# French Withdrawal Causation Debate

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.161 begins a causation discussion that appears to qualify U.S.-pressure explanations of French withdrawal; pp.184-185 continue the argument by treating withdrawal as unavoidable after the U.S. Civil War while not making U.S.-French war the only explanation.
