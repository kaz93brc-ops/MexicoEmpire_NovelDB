# State Governors Tutelage

## Historical Role

Theme for Hamnett's argument that Juárez depended on state governors' armed power in the Reform War while also seeking to free his government from their tutelage.

## Creative Use

Use to make Juárez's provincial support a political constraint, not simply a loyal backing force.

## Open Questions

- Identify the governors, state forces, and negotiations Hamnett has in mind after p.83's unfinished sentence is resolved.
- Confirm whether "tutelage" is Hamnett's wording or a compact DB label for the relationship.
