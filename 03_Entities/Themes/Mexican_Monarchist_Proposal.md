## Hamnett 1994 p.169 Monarchist-Base Warning Notes

Hamnett p.169 records that Napoleon III was moving toward a Mexican empire concept by at least October 1861, while Miramón, Prim, and later Bazaine warned that Mexico lacked a real monarchist party.
