Hamnett pp.202-203 develops the later Juárez presidency through the 14 August 1867 Convocatoria, the shift from defending the 1857 Constitution to seeking its reform, the use of extraordinary powers against dissenting state governors, and the practical reliance on caudillos, caciques, and factions.
