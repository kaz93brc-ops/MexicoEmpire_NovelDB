# Bazaine Political Military Strategy

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.177 treats Bazaine as a political-military challenger to Juárez: he advanced militarily while trying to draw the political middle ground away from the republican government.

## Creative Use

Use Bazaine as a calculating French commander who can support Maximilian while limiting imperial autonomy and contesting Juárez's support base.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the documentary source for Bazaine's dislike of Conservatives and Miramón.
- Confirm evidence for delaying Maximilian's independent imperial army.
- Confirm whether the political-middle-ground strategy is Hamnett's synthesis or a source-derived Bazaine objective.
