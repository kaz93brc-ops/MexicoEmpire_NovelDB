## Hamnett 1994 p.120 Customs Revenue Notes

Hamnett p.120 adds a Reform War finance problem: Liberal access to ports and border revenues helped offset battlefield disadvantage, but customs revenue often remained in the hands of state governors or regional power holders rather than Juárez's central government.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.136-137 Chihuahua Fiscal Notes

Hamnett pp.136-137 adds Chihuahua-specific war finance: Terrazas used proceeds and federal revenues locally, the federal government lost control of northern customs revenues, and Trías's governorship temporarily remitted those revenues to the federal government.

## Hamnett 1994 p.167 External Debt Crisis Notes

Hamnett p.167 adds a post-Reform War fiscal crisis: Veracruz customs revenue was heavily assigned to foreign creditors, Ocampo warned against overestimating Church-property revenue, and the cabinet hesitated over Lerdo's foreign-debt suspension proposal.
