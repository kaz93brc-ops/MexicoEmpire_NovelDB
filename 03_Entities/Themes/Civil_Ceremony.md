# Civil Ceremony

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.100-101 presents Juárez's Oaxaca inauguration as a civil ceremony after the failed third Te Deum and closed cathedral doors.

## Creative Use

Civil ceremony can show republican authority continuing without cathedral sanction.
