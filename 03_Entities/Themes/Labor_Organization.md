# Labor Organization

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.216 connects early factory worker organization to the July 1868 Mexico City textile strike, the September 1870 Gran Círculo de Obreros de México, 1871 branches, and 1872 pro-administration Liberal infiltration.

## Creative Use

Use as the theme hub for Restored Republic worker organizing, government monitoring, radical influence, and links between Zacatecas protection and Mexico City labor politics.
