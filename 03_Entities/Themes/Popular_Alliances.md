# Popular Alliances

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.57 depicts Alvarez as a builder of flexible popular alliances that included lower-class and ethnic groups, while also sometimes anticipating and containing their autonomous radicalization.

## Creative Use

Use to portray popular mobilization as both a resource and a threat within Liberal politics.
