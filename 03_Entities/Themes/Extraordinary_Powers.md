# Extraordinary Powers

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.119 treats extraordinary powers as authority granted to Comonfort by the dissolved Congress on 1857-11-05 and inherited by Juárez until he returned them to the elected Congress on 1861-05-09.

## Creative Use

Use to show Juárez's constitutional legitimacy and emergency executive power as a tension rather than a simple rule-of-law story.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the 1857-11-05 grant, its legal wording, and the mechanism by which Juárez inherited the powers.
- Confirm the 1861-05-09 return to Congress and its political meaning after the Reform War.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.202-203 Convocatoria Notes

Hamnett pp.202-203 identifies the legal basis of the 1867 Convocatoria as extraordinary powers still held from the last sitting Congress, identified here as the 1863 Congress, and then shows Juárez using those powers against Puebla and Guanajuato governors who refused publication.
