# Military Threat To Constitutional Order

## Historical Role

Theme for Hamnett's argument that powerful military commanders threatened constitutional order, visible in the army's role in the Tacubaya crisis and Comonfort's collapse.

## Creative Use

Use to connect Juárez's constitutionalism to concrete civil-military danger rather than abstract legalism alone.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the commanders, units, and sequence by which the army first enabled Comonfort and then turned against him.
- Separate Hamnett's lifetime-warning framing from primary-source evidence of Juárez's own words.
