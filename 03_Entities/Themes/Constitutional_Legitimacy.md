## Hamnett 1994 p.84 Notes

Hamnett p.84 adds a compact legitimacy claim: the overthrow of constitutional order made defense of the 1857 Constitution a Liberal moral justification, but that same Constitution later constrained Juárez.

## Hamnett 1994 p.122-123 Notes

Hamnett pp.122-123 makes constitutional legitimacy the core reason Juárez opposed mediation: Lerdo's proposal and Mathews's diplomatic plan threatened to remove Juárez and replace the constitutional presidency through a new settlement or provisional president.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.220-221 Re-election Notes
