# Developmentalism

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.65 associates Miguel Lerdo with a view that saw corporate ownership as an obstacle to development and sought a radical transformation of land relations.

## Creative Use

Use to distinguish Lerdo's economic-development logic from Juarez's broader reform symbolism.

## Open Questions

- Confirm Lerdo's own language for development and Juarez's degree of agreement.
