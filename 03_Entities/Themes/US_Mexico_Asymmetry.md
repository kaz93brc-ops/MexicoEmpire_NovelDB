# US-Mexico Asymmetry

## Historical Role

Theme hub for Hamnett's p.162-p.163 conclusion that Mexico and the United States were inseparable but unequal neighbors shaped by Mexico's nineteenth-century decline and U.S. continental rise.

## Creative Use

Use to keep U.S.-Mexico scenes from becoming simple friendship or simple hostility; the relationship is unavoidable, unequal, and politically useful but frustrating.

## Open Questions

- Distinguish Hamnett's interpretation from Romero's public optimism and from later Mexican suspicion of U.S. expansionism.
