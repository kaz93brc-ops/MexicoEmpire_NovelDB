# Fueros

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.87 notes that the 1824 Federal Constitution retained Church and army fueros, keeping corporate privilege inside early republican constitutional order.

## Creative Use

Use this as an institutional target of Liberal Reform, not as a vague dislike of religion or soldiers.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the constitutional provisions and later reform measures that altered ecclesiastical and military jurisdiction.
