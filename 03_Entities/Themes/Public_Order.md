# Public Order

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.101 shows Reform authorities framing certain clerical actions as public-order problems, especially pulpit politics and the Zoochila priest's refusal of last rites and Christian burial over the constitutional oath.

## Creative Use

Public order can show why Liberal officials justified intervention in religious practice without presenting the action as simple anti-religion.
