## Hamnett 1994 p.193 Body Custody Notes

Hamnett p.193 says Juárez refused to hand over Maximilian's body, the Mexican government controlled embalming and guard, and Vice-Admiral Tegetthoff arrived on 1867-09-01 but failed to obtain the body at that stage.
