## Hamnett 1994 p.169 Intervention Notes

Hamnett p.169 treats Prim's appointment as commander of the [[Spanish_Expeditionary_Force|Spanish Expeditionary Force]] as evidence that the [[ODonnell_Government|O'Donnell government]] had moved away from a French regime-change project, and also groups Prim among those who warned that Mexico lacked a real monarchist party.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.194-195 Relations with Spain Notes

Hamnett pp.194-195 says that after the Bourbon monarchy collapsed in Spain in 1868, Prim hoped to renew relations with Mexico, though restoration did not occur until June 1871.
