# US-Mexico Relations

## Definition

1830年代以降、Mexicoの外交・内政に継続的影響を与えた対米関係。

## Hamnett 1994 pp.154-155 Civil War Diplomacy Notes

Hamnett pp.154-155 deepens the Chapter 7 caution: Juárez and Romero sought U.S. aid, but Seward and the Lincoln administration avoided European war risks, loan discussions collapsed, and Corwin's later proposal carried mortgage and sovereignty hazards.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.162-163 Chapter 7 Conclusion Notes

Hamnett pp.162-163 closes Chapter 7 by treating U.S.-Mexico relations as unavoidable but unequal: U.S. policy prioritized France and European balance-of-power concerns, Romero's public optimism needed qualification, and Juárez's constitutional hopes coexisted with short-term disappointment.
