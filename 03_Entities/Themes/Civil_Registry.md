# Civil Registry

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.98 links Civil Registry formation to the Lafragua Law of 1857-06-27 as part of Comonfort-era reform legislation during Juárez's absence from the capital.

Hamnett p.106 presents the Veracruz program as removing birth, marriage, and death registration from clerical jurisdiction and placing them in the Civil Registry.

Hamnett p.109 states that a Civil Registry system was established in Liberal-held territory on 1859-07-28, and that Juárez registered his newborn son in the Veracruz Registry.

## Creative Use

Use as an administrative reform that moves family status and civil life away from exclusive ecclesiastical control.
