## Hamnett 1994 pp.222-223 Félix Díaz Control Notes

Hamnett pp.222-223 makes Oaxaca politics central to the post-1867 Juárez-Díaz conflict: Félix Díaz displaced juarista control through the 1867 governorship election, jefes políticos, the state government, La Victoria, anticlerical measures, and the National Guard, while Castro, Romero, Mauleón, sierra militia leaders, and Ixtlán mining interests show that the conflict remained a Liberal internal struggle.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.224-225 Juchitán and La Noria Notes

Hamnett pp.224-225 makes Oaxaca politics a hinge between Juárez's earlier state-building tools and anti-Juárez Liberal revolt: the National Guard, Juchitán resistance, Félix Díaz's state power, the 1871 electoral break, and the Plan of La Noria all converge in the state.
