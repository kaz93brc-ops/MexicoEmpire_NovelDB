## Hamnett 1994 pp.154-155 Debt and U.S. Posture Notes

Hamnett pp.154-155 ties European intervention to Mexico's debt suspension, the London Convention, British settlement efforts, U.S. refusal to join debt collection, U.S. recognition of European debt-collection rights, and U.S. rejection of European territorial acquisition.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.168-169 Allied Motives and French Design Notes

Hamnett pp.168-169 separates British, Spanish, and French motives after identifying the 17 July 1861 debt suspension as the direct cause: Britain is treated mainly through debt, Spain through prestige and U.S. counterweight concerns, and France through Jecker claims and Napoleon III's monarchy project.
