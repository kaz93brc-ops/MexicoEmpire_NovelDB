# United States Recognition Issue

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.148-149 frames U.S. recognition or non-recognition as a central issue for Mexican political legitimacy, loans, Veracruz security, and foreign intervention during the Reform War.

## Creative Use

Use this theme to hold the tension between Juárez's need for U.S. recognition and the danger of U.S. territorial and transit demands.

## Open Questions

- Confirm Chapter 7 notes 1-6 and compare this recognition issue with later U.S. non-recognition of Maximilian's government.
