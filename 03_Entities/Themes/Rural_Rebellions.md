## Hamnett 1994 p.66-67 Notes

Hamnett p.66-67 adds Reform-era rural rebellions tied to Lerdo Law implementation, including multiple states, Tepic under Manuel Lozada, the Cuernavaca/Cuautla sugar zone, and Tlaxiaco after Oaxaca implementation.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.214-215 Restored Republic Peasant Resistance Notes

Hamnett pp.214-215 presents the 1868 Chávez López rebellion and its spread through central Mexico as peasant resistance to the reintroduced Liberal order after imperial protection procedures were dismantled.
