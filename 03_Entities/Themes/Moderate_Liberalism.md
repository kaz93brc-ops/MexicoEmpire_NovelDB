## Hamnett 1994 p.199 Later Juárez and Moderate Opinion Notes

Hamnett p.199 presents moderate Liberal and moderate-opinion problems as central to the later Juárez: constitutional skepticism, fear of lower-class electoral influence, Juárez's constitutional approach to moderates, Maximilian's temporary consensus appeal, and the December 1866 conservative turn.
