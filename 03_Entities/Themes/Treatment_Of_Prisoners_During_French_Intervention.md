# Treatment Of Prisoners During The French Intervention

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.179 presents prisoner treatment as uneven because the opposing sides did not formally recognize one another as belligerents, leaving local commanders to improvise.

## Creative Use

Use to contrast republican legality, battlefield severity, and local mercy without turning either side into a simple moral type.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the source trail for Escobedo's summary executions and Riva Palacio's lenient handling in Michoacán.
- Check whether local arrangements left written records or appear mainly in later reputations.
