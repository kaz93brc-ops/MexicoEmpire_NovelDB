# Executive Power Vs Congress

## Historical Role

Theme for Comonfort-era disputes over extraordinary powers, constitutional revision, congressional authority, and rule by executive decree.

## Creative Use

Use to avoid flattening Comonfort's crisis into personal betrayal; the conflict is institutional as well as factional.

## Open Questions

- Confirm which requests for extraordinary powers were made, rejected, and later granted under pressure.
- Distinguish Hamnett interpretation from official decrees and congressional records.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.202-203 Convocatoria Notes

Hamnett pp.202-203 presents the 1867 Convocatoria as Juárez and Lerdo's attempt to prevent a return to the 1857 and 1861-1863 pattern of legislative predominance by revising the constitutional balance, chiefly through bicameralism.
