## Hamnett 1994 pp.170-171 Confederacy Timing Notes

Hamnett pp.170-171 connects South Carolina's secession, Confederate formation, and the American Civil War to Napoleon III's opening for Mexico, then argues that the Mexican Empire's viability seemed tied to Confederate viability after the Union victories of July 1863.
