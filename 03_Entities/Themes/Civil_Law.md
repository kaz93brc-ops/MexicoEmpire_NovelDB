# Civil Law

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.91 frames Governor Juárez's position as requiring clergy to obey federal and state law and not place corporate status above civil law.

## Creative Use

Use to write Juárez's church policy as a legal-jurisdictional conflict rather than a simple campaign against belief.
