# Tierras Baldías

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.73 links tierras baldías to the powers of jefes políticos, who were involved in local administration, disputed elections, army recruitment, and land management under state-level political structures.

## Creative Use

Use this theme to show how liberal state-building, local administration, land classification, and regional power could intersect without reducing local society to criminality or backwardness.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the legal meaning of tierras baldías in mid-nineteenth-century Mexico and how the term differed from communal land, public land, or private property.
- Confirm what powers jefes políticos had over these lands in law and practice.
