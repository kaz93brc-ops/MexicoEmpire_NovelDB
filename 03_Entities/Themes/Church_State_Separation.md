# Church-State Separation

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.101 interprets Juárez's handling of the Oaxaca Cathedral conflict as establishing that civil power should not adopt a particular religion and that public office could be separated from official religious ceremony.

Hamnett p.106-107 shows that the Manifesto and the 1859 Veracruz law moved this principle into explicit Church-state separation and limited government protection of Catholic worship to the same footing as other religions.

## Creative Use

Use this theme where Juárez is not denying private religion but refusing religious ceremony as a condition of public authority.
