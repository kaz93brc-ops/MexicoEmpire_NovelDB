## Historical Role

## Hamnett 1994 p.91 Notes

Hamnett p.91 reports Sierra's view that the Juárez Law was a revolutionary act grounded in equality before the law, then keeps that interpretation separate from Hamnett's more cautious assessment of Juárez's radicalism.

## Hamnett 1994 p.96-97 Notes

Hamnett pp.96-97 emphasizes the law's jurisdictional limits: it restricted exemptions from civil law, removed ecclesiastical and military courts from civil cases, and left internal discipline to those courts. Hamnett also treats the measure as more moderate at enactment than Juárez's later self-memory suggested.
