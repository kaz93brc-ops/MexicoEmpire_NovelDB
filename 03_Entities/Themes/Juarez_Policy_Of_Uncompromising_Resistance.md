# Juarez Policy Of Uncompromising Resistance

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.175-177 shows Juárez responding to European intervention through emergency powers and laws that treated collaboration with intervention forces as treason or capital collaboration, then extends that policy to imperial officeholders, confiscation, and the later Maximilian death-demand logic.

## Creative Use

Use as a frame for the tension between sovereignty defense and the coercive legal instruments of republican resistance.
