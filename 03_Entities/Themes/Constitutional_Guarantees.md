# Constitutional Guarantees

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.130 records Congress suspending constitutional guarantees on 3 May 1862 until the new session on 16 September 1862 or the end of armed conflict.

## Creative Use

Use to show foreign intervention pushing the republican government into emergency constitutional measures rather than simple peacetime legality.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the legal form, wording, and scope of the 3 May 1862 suspension.
- Confirm how the suspension relates to extraordinary faculties and congressional authority.
