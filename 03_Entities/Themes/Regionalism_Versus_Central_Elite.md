# Regionalism Versus Central Elite

## Definition

Theme for liberalism as a language of regional sentiment and resistance to the traditional dominance of Mexico's central elite.

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.49 presents Mexican liberalism not only as imported ideology but also as a doctrine that could express regional feeling and resistance to the centre-core elite.
