## Hamnett 1994 p.89 Chapter 5 Notes

Hamnett p.89 presents Pius IX as reading Mexican Liberalism through the wider Catholic-world conflict over nationalism, national churches, and state power, including an analogy between Mexican Liberals and Mazzini / Garibaldi.

## Hamnett 1994 p.99 Allocution Notes

Hamnett p.99 summarizes Pius IX's 1856-12-15 Allocution as condemning the Juárez Law, Lerdo Law, Labastida's banishment, Puebla diocesan property sequestration, clergy disenfranchisement, anti-religious constitutional draft articles, and civil procedures that ignored the Holy See and ecclesiastical authorities.
