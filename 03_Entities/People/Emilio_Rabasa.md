# Emilio Rabasa

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.73-74 presents Rabasa as a later critic of the 1857 Constitution, especially on executive-legislative imbalance, universal suffrage, and the political standing of constitutional deputies.

## Creative Use

Use Rabasa as a retrospective constitutional critic, not as a direct 1857 participant or as the DB's own verdict on popular politics.
