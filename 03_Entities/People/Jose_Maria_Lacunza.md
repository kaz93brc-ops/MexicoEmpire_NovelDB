# José María Lacunza

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.187 places Lacunza in Maximilian's final institutional planning: abdication handling if Maximilian were captured and membership in the February 1867 Regency Council.

## Creative Use

Use as a figure for Maximilian's attempt to preserve legal form during imperial collapse, after checking the original appointment and Lacunza's exact office.
