# Julio Chávez López

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.214-215 presents Julio Chávez López as a central figure in the 1868 Chalco-area peasant rebellion, with a background as a hacienda tenant and Liberal army soldier.

## Creative Use

Use as a figure through whom expectations of Liberal arbitration, agrarian pressure, and federal military repression can be staged without reducing peasant resistance to crime.
