# Ignacio Altamirano

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.54 uses Altamirano as a contrast to Juárez, who is portrayed as an administrator rather than a literary nationalist.

## Creative Use

Use as a contrast point when distinguishing Liberal cultural nationalism from Juárez's administrative political style.
