# José Agustín Domínguez

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.100-101 presents Domínguez as Bishop of Oaxaca during Juárez's 1856-1857 governorship. He performed or allowed Te Deum ceremonies while objecting to constitutional provisions he judged contrary to canon law, then became part of the dispute over the failed third Te Deum.

## Creative Use

Domínguez can show that the Church side was not a single will: a bishop could avoid immediate rupture while cathedral clergy and constitutional conflict narrowed his room for compromise.
