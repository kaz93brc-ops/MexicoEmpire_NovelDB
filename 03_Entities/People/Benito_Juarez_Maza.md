# Benito Juarez Maza

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.234 presents Benito Juarez Maza as a son of Benito Juarez and Margarita Maza who served as governor of Oaxaca in 1911-1912.

## Creative Use

Use him as a long-afterlife link between the Juarez family and Oaxaca politics, not as direct evidence for the 1872 succession crisis.
