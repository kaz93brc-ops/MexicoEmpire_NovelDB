# Antonio Mantecón

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.92 presents Dr Antonio Mantecón as the bishop who arrived in Oaxaca in July 1844, came from an influential Hispanic family in the state, and administered the diocese until his death in February 1852.

## Creative Use

Use him as a local ecclesiastical counterpart to Governor Juárez, especially for scenes of negotiation over schools, clergy discipline, and civil authority.

## Open Questions

- Confirm full name, accents, career, Santa Anna's role in presenting him, and note 10.
- Confirm how Mantecón viewed Juárez's Zapotec origin, masonic affiliation, and Liberal politics; Hamnett says the sources do not reveal this.

## Hamnett 1994 p.94-95 Notes

Hamnett pp.94-95 presents Mantecon as both collaborator and opponent: he encouraged clergy cooperation on Tehuantepec-Huatulco road works, complained about Zimatlan parish dues, resisted Juarez's hospital and Hospice intervention through the Ruiz exchange, and warned that church-property statistics could alarm Indigenous communities.
