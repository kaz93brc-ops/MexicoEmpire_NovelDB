# Ignacio Romero Vargas

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.204 identifies Ignacio Romero Vargas as Puebla governor after Rafael J. García and links him to the attempt to restrain autonomous sierra chieftains.

## Creative Use

Use as a Puebla state official caught between formal governorship and highland military-local authority.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.210-211 Notes

Hamnett pp.210-211 treats Romero Vargas as García's successor whose unpopularity helped prevent federal support recovery in Puebla, and whose civil authority Juárez removed when Puebla was placed under siege on 5 March 1872.
