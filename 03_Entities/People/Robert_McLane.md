# Robert McLane

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.150-151 presents McLane as Buchanan's envoy who recognized the Juárez regime on 1859-04-06 and pressed for territorial, transit, and financial concessions that led into the McLane-Ocampo Treaty.

## Creative Use

Use him as the diplomatic pressure point where recognition, loans, Lower California, Tehuantepec, and U.S. intervention clauses are negotiated.
