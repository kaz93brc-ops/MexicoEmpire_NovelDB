# Félix Díaz

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.204 presents Félix Díaz as the figure to whom juaristas lost Oaxaca in the 1867 state elections, and whose adherence to his brother's Rebellion of Noria preceded his fall and violent death in early 1872.

## Creative Use

Use as an Oaxaca state-power rival showing that post-Intervention republican politics remained factional inside the Liberal world.

## Open Questions

- Confirm offices, 1867 Oaxaca election details, precise relation to Porfirio Díaz, and exact death date.
- Confirm the documentary basis for linking his fall to the Rebellion of Noria.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.222-223 Oaxaca Control Notes

Hamnett pp.222-223 treats Félix Díaz, El Chato, as the central figure in Oaxaca state control from the 1867 governorship election to the La Noria crisis. The spread links his 1867 victory over Miguel Castro, use of `jefes políticos`, control of `La Victoria`, anti-clerical measures, appointment machine, 1869 legislative majority, National Guard reconstruction, and roughly 3,000 armed men by 1871.
