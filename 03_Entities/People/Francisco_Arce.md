# Francisco Arce

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.204 states that General Francisco Arce was placed between armed Álvarez and Jiménez factions in Guerrero.

## Creative Use

Use as a federal-military mediator in a state conflict that cannot be reduced to Liberal versus Conservative politics.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.212-213 Guerrero Civil War Notes

Hamnett pp.212-213 presents Arce as Juárez's outside mediator in Guerrero, ordered against Álvarez's private force, resisted through state-legislature constitutional claims, forced out by June 1870, then restored after Juárez turned back to Álvarez.
