# Vicente Jiménez

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.200-201 presents General Vicente Jiménez as a Guerrero regional figure whose hostility with Diego Álvarez's faction destabilized the state after the French Intervention.

## Creative Use

Use as a Guerrero provincial power figure showing that republican victory did not dissolve local rivalries.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.212-213 Guerrero Civil War Notes

Hamnett pp.212-213 completes the "blamed ..." sentence as Jiménez supporters accusing Juárez over Álvarez family dominance, then follows the Jiménez faction through Altamirano sympathy, National Guard pressure in Tixtla and Chilapa, the temporary Arce reversal, a press reply to El Siglo XIX, and the transition toward porfirista opposition.

Hamnett p.214 resolves the p.213 continuation by stating that Jiménez organized porfirista forces in Guerrero, coastal Oaxaca, and toward Putla while seeking to join Félix Díaz during the Rebellion of La Noria.
