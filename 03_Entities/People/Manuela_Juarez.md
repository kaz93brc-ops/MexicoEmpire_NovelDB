# Manuela Juarez

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.51 identifies Manuela "Nelita" Juarez as Juarez's eldest daughter and notes her May 1863 marriage to Pedro Santacilia.

## Creative Use

Her marriage can connect the Juarez household to Cuban exile networks without turning the family into a simple political symbol.
