# Pedro Ogazón

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.217 places Pedro Ogazón among Lozada's enemies who were temporarily put in the same Liberal camp after Juárez accepted Lozada's recognition in July 1867.

## Creative Use

Use as a Jalisco Liberal counterpart whose conflict with Lozada can show how Liberal reform, state authority, and community rights collided.
