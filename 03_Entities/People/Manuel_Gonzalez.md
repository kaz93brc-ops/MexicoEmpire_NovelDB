# Manuel González

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.227 presents Manuel González as Porfirio Díaz's intermediary to northern generals and Puebla sierra leaders during the La Noria rebellion, and as a former Conservative who became personally loyal to Díaz.

## Creative Use

Use as a connector figure who shows how Díaz's rebellion could draw on personal loyalty and cross-factional networks rather than a simple Liberal/Conservative divide.
