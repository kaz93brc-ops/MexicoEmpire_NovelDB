# Justo Sierra

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.52 uses Sierra as part of the historiography that placed Ocampo in a more radical intellectual position than Juárez.

## Creative Use

Use as a historiographical voice, not as an in-scene actor for the 1850s.

## Hamnett 1994 p.90-91 Notes

Hamnett p.90-91 summarizes Sierra's interpretation of Ocampo's circle as Jacobins and of the Juárez Law as a revolutionary act grounded in equality before the law, then separates that view from Hamnett's own reluctance to label Juárez a Jacobin at this stage.
