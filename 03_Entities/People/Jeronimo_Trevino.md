# Jerónimo Treviño

## Historical Role

Nuevo León出身の軍事指揮官。Hamnett p.131では、Mariano Escobedo、Ignacio ZaragozaとともにVidaurriから離れ、Juárez陣営へ明確に移った人物として扱われる。

## Creative Use

Use as part of the military realignment from Vidaurri's north-eastern base toward Juárez's constitutional government.

## Hamnett 1994 p.227 La Noria Notes

Hamnett p.227 identifies Treviño as Nuevo León governor and says he rebelled against Juárez on 29 September 1871, denouncing corruption and betrayed 1867 expectations while invoking constitutional legality and state sovereignty.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.230-231 Treviño Rebellion Endgame Notes

Hamnett pp.230-231 says Treviño threatened San Luis Potosí and the Bajío with 8,000 men in January 1872, before Rocha entered Monterrey on 9 July and ended the rebellion.
