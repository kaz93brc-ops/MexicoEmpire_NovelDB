# Trinidad García de la Cadena

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.215-216 introduces General Trinidad García de la Cadena as an anti-Juárez rebel whose political base lay in Zacatecas; p.216 links that base to radical federalism, González Ortega politics, and support for urban and peasant movements.

## Creative Use

Use as a political-rebellion counterpart to agrarian unrest, showing that the Juárez government faced multiple kinds of post-1867 opposition at once.

## Open Questions

## Hamnett 1994 pp.216 Labor And Regionalism Notes

Hamnett p.216 connects Cadena with Negrete, Zacatecas federalist regionalism, the unresolved 1868 rebellion, and protection of early factory worker organization after the July 1868 Mexico City textile strike.
