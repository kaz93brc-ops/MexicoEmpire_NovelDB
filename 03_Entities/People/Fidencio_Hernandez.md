# Fidencio Hernández

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.222 identifies Fidencio Hernández, with Francisco Meijueiro, as controlling Oaxaca sierra militias that were mainly loyal to Miguel Castro and Juárez.

## Creative Use

Use as a sierra militia-power figure who complicates any simple picture of Díaz dominance in Oaxaca.
