# Pedro Santacilia

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.51 introduces Santacilia as a Cuban separatist who became important during Juarez's New Orleans exile and later married Manuela "Nelita" Juarez.

## Creative Use

Use Santacilia as a bridge between Juarez's exile, Cuban exile circles, and later republican family networks.

## Open Questions

- Details of the 1852 attempted transfer to Spain, Gibraltar passage, and escape to New York.
- Exact source, date, and place of the 1863 marriage to Manuela Juarez.
- Scope and political meaning of Juarez's "Cuban connection" after p.51.
