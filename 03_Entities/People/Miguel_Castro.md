# Miguel Castro

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.20-21で、Mejueiro familyとともに北部山地を支配し、1870年代までjuarista causeを支えた人物として言及される。

Hamnett p.36では、1848年9月時点のOaxaca州議会構成員の一人としても挙げられる。北部山地の支持基盤と州議会エリート層の接点として要確認。

## Hamnett 1994 p.222 Election and Sierra Militia Notes

Hamnett p.222 identifies Miguel Castro as the juarista candidate defeated by Félix Díaz in the 10 November 1867 Oaxaca governorship election, while also noting Castro's continuing strength in sierra bases and the main loyalty of sierra militias under Francisco Meijueiro and Fidencio Hernández.

Hamnett pp.230-231 presents Castro as the figure Juárez wanted restored in Oaxaca, Alatorre nominated as provisional governor, and the federal government appointed on 16 January 1872; Hamnett also links Castro to Félix Díaz's ruin.
