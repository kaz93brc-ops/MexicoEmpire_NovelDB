# Florencio Villarreal

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.57 identifies Florencio Villarreal as the Costa Chica civil governor and military commander who proclaimed the Plan of Ayutla in the Ometepec district on 1854-03-01.

## Creative Use

Use as the local declarant through whom the Ayutla pronouncement moved from Alvarez-Comonfort planning into public regional action.
