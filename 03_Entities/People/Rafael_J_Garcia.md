# Rafael J. García

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.204 identifies Rafael J. García as Puebla governor in the post-1867 period and links him to efforts to restrain the autonomous power of sierra chieftains.

## Creative Use

Use as a state-government figure trying to translate central authority into control over Puebla highland power.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.208-209 Puebla Notes

Hamnett pp.208-209 presents García as the juarista Puebla governor opposed by Méndez, weakly supported beyond Rafael Cravioto, and viewed by opponents around Zacapoaxtla as an executive imposition contrary to the Constitution.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.210-211 Notes

Hamnett pp.210-211 links García's enforcement of the 1863 law on uncultivated land to Huauchinango-area village and land disputes, and later treats García and Romero Vargas as governors with weak Puebla support against sierra chiefs.
