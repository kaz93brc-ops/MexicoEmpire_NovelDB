# Jesús Carranza

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.180 presents Jesús Carranza as a Coahuila-based republican supporter whom Juárez recommended to Escobedo and who soon became jefe político of Monclova.

Hamnett Chapter 8 note 21, mapped in [[CAP_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0101|CAP_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_0101]], adds that Jesús Carranza was the father of Venustiano Carranza; this remains a peripheral genealogy note pending outside confirmation.

## Creative Use

Use as a local republican connector between Juárez, Escobedo, Coahuila politics, and later Carranza-family questions without collapsing him into later figures.

## Open Questions

- Confirm full name, family position, and the Venustiano Carranza parentage note outside Hamnett.
- Confirm the date and legal basis of the Monclova jefe político appointment.
