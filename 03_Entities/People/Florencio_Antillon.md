# Florencio Antillón

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.203 identifies Florencio Antillón as Guanajuato governor who refused to publish the 14 August 1867 Convocatoria and was removed by Juárez using extraordinary powers.

## Creative Use

Use as a state-level Liberal actor whose resistance to publication makes central executive intervention visible after the empire's collapse.
