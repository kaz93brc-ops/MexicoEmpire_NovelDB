## Hamnett 1994 p.223 Juárez Information Network Notes

Hamnett p.223 presents Félix Romero as a central juarista figure in Oaxaca politics. Juárez sought to have him elected president of the state Supreme Court to secure succession leverage, and from 7 January 1870 Romero acted as governor's secretary inside the Díaz administration while serving as a Juárez-side information source.
