# Guadalupe Victoria

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.220-221 names Guadalupe Victoria as one of the few Mexican presidents before 1867 to complete a formal presidential term, using him as context for why Juárez's later re-elections appeared unusual.

## Creative Use

Use as a benchmark for the rarity of completed terms and peaceful succession in nineteenth-century Mexican political memory.
