# Juan Francisco Lucas

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.204 presents Juan Francisco Lucas as a Puebla sierra chieftain whose autonomous power and National Guard contingents concerned Puebla state governors.

## Creative Use

Use as a regional power figure showing that National Guard forces could support local autonomy rather than simple central order.
