# Daniel Cosío Villegas

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.246–249 presents Cosío Villegas as a critic of constitutional determinism and as a historian locating modern Mexico's origins in Reforma and Díaz-era modernization as well as the 1910 Revolution.

## Creative Use

Use as a historiographical counterweight to simple Juárez-versus-Díaz and Revolution-only origin stories.

## Open Questions
