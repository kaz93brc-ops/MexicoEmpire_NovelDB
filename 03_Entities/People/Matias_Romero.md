# Matías Romero

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.75 presents Matías Romero as a young Oaxacan linked to Juárez, working in the Ministry of Foreign Relations and relaying Mexico City political and diplomatic information to Juárez in Oaxaca.

## Creative Use

Use Romero as the information channel through which Juárez follows Comonfort-era politics from Oaxaca without turning Juárez into an omnipresent capital politician.

## Open Questions

- Confirm Romero's education, ministry position, correspondence, and the original Juárez-Romero letters.
- Confirm the dates and content of Romero's reports on El Siglo XIX, Liberal factional tension, and ministerial changes.
