# George Mathews

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.123 identifies George Mathews as the British Chargé d'Affaires in Mexico City who was in contact with Santos Degollado and, from 1860-09-18, proposed mediation through the Mexico City diplomatic corps.

## Creative Use

Use him as the diplomatic channel through which mediation could threaten Juárez's presidency and constitutional legitimacy, pending confirmation of title, documents, and p.123 continuation.
