# Francois-Xavier Guerra

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.50-51 summarizes Guerra as an interpreter of Mexican Liberalism, dictatorship, and the Diaz regime.

Hamnett p.86 also notes Guerra's attention to masonic lodges as connection points in the Reforma and Restored Republic periods.

## Creative Use

Use as a historiographical caution against treating Liberalism as uniformly popular or uniformly imposed.
