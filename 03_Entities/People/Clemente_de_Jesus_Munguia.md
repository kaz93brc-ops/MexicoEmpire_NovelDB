# Clemente de Jesús Munguía

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.96-97 presents Munguía as Bishop of Michoacán who challenged the civil authority to limit the ecclesiastical fuero after the Juárez Law.

## Creative Use

Use him to voice the Church-side jurisdictional argument without turning the whole Catholic Church into a single actor.
