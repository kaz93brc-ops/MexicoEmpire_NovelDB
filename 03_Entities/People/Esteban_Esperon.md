# Esteban Esperón

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.67 describes Esteban Esperón as a major sugar producer near Tlaxiaco and as the brother of José Esperón; he sought permission in 1856 to privatize plantation lands long leased from a local Indigenous community.

## Creative Use

Use as a local landholder whose family, Liberal factional ties, and land claims expose the rural stakes of the Lerdo Law.
