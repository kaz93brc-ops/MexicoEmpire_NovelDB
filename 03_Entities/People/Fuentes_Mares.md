# Fuentes Mares

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.90 cites Fuentes Mares as an interpretive voice on the moral and quasi-priestly quality of Reform liberalism and Juárez's black clothing.

## Creative Use

Use as a historiographical voice for the austere moral image of Reform leaders, not as direct evidence of Juárez's private intention.
