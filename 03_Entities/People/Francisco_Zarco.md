# Francisco Zarco

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.55 reports Guzmán's view that Zarco helped preserve the progressive color of Juárez's cabinet after the 1861 remodeling.

## Creative Use

Use as part of the progressive cabinet cluster rather than as a fully developed character until further sources are checked.

## Hamnett 1994 p.112 Reform Laws Defense Notes

Hamnett p.112 records Zarco's 1861-01-20 defense of the Reform Laws as a claim that the laws were not hostility to religion but a way to free the Church from state and party politics.
