# Manuel Ruiz

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.82 identifies Manuel Ruiz as an Oaxacan client of Juárez whom Juárez persuaded Comonfort to appoint as Minister of Justice in the 20 October 1857 cabinet.

## Creative Use

Use as a concrete cabinet-placement example of Juárez's Oaxaca network inside Comonfort-era national politics.

Hamnett p.95 identifies Manuel Ruiz as Juarez's secretary in the hospital-repair dispute with Mantecon; Ruiz was initially refused access, returned with a written assertion of public duty and a city council deputation, and then framed the bishop's conduct as disrespect toward civil authority.

## Hamnett 1994 p.108-109 Notes

Hamnett pp.108-109 continues Ruiz's circular defense of the Veracruz Reform Laws: Ruiz framed confiscation as separating religion from clerical abuse and corporate privilege, denied that the Church required state protection as a complete association, and justified civil marriage through clerical refusal of marriage sacraments to constitutional oath-takers.
