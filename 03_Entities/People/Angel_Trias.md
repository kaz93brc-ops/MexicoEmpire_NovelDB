# Angel Trías

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.137 identifies Angel Trías as the general Patoni appointed governor of Chihuahua from June 1864 to July 1865 after the attempt to displace Luis Terrazas.

## Creative Use

Use as a fragile replacement figure whose appointment did not overcome Terrazas's local base.
