# Francisco Serrano

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.52 states that Serrano sought to make Cuba a platform for Spanish re-expansion in the Americas during the 1850s.

## Creative Use

Use as the Spanish-imperial counterpoint to Mexican Liberal and Cuban exile anxieties about Cuba.
