Hamnett p.169 presents Montluc as the Mexican consul in [[Paris]], a supporter of [[Benito_Juarez|Benito Juárez]], and a contact of [[French_Opposition_to_Napoleon_III|French republican opposition]] who warned against French intervention in Mexico.
