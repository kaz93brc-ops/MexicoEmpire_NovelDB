# Roberto Maqueo

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.223 presents Roberto Maqueo as a businessman connected to the sale of Juárez's Oaxaca City house and as a figure with links to both juarista and Díaz circles.

## Creative Use

Use as a pragmatic intermediary in Oaxaca, useful for scenes where commercial interests and factional politics overlap.
