# Sóstenes Rocha

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.204-205 lists Sóstenes Rocha among the army officers to whom Juárez delegated authority in response to local or provincial rebellions.

## Creative Use

## Hamnett 1994 p.227 Tampico Notes

Hamnett p.227 says Rocha suppressed the Tampico garrison uprising in May-June 1871, one of the failed openings that weakened southern prospects for Díaz's rebellion.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.230-231 Northern Rebellion Suppression Notes

Hamnett pp.230-231 presents Rocha as taking Aguascalientes on 29 February 1872, defeating Zacatecas rebels three days later, entering Monterrey on 9 July, and serving as one of the effective commanders available to Juárez.
