# J. J. Casavantes

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.137 identifies J. J. Casavantes as Juárez's preferred candidate for the Chihuahua governorship, but notes that he lacked a local base in the state.

## Creative Use

Use as a weak central-government candidate who shows the limits of Juárez's control over Chihuahua politics.
