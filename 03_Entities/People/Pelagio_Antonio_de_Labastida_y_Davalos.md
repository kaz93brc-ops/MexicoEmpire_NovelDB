## Hamnett 1994 p.97 Juárez Law Protest Notes

Hamnett p.97 records Labastida, then Bishop of Puebla, protesting the Juárez Law on 1855-11-29; Hamnett notes that the protest was not extreme in expression, while Puebla unrest exceeded episcopal control.
