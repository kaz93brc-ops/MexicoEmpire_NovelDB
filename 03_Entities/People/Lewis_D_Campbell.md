# Lewis D. Campbell

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.188 identifies Campbell as the U.S. Minister to Mexico appointed in October 1866 and still in New Orleans when Seward transmitted Austrian intercession concerns for Maximilian's safety.

## Creative Use

Use Campbell as a diplomatic relay whose warning to Lerdo turns international clemency pressure into a direct sovereignty argument.
