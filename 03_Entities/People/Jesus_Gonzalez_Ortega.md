## Hamnett 1994 p.103 Notes

Hamnett p.103 places Jesús González Ortega among Jalisco/Zacatecas radical Liberal leaders and Liberal Army commanders. He is associated with prohibiting religious processions, legalizing civil marriage, and issuing a 1859-07-16 measure against clergy who pressured constitutional oath-takers or denied sacraments.
