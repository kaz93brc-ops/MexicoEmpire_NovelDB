# Francisco Meijueiro

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.222 places Francisco Meijueiro among the figures controlling Oaxaca sierra militias that were mainly loyal to Miguel Castro and Juárez rather than personally loyal to the Díaz brothers.

## Creative Use

Use as a named sierra militia leader showing that Oaxaca armed power was not automatically available to Félix or Porfirio Díaz.
