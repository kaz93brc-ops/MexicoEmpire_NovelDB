# Juan N. Méndez

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.203 identifies Juan N. Méndez as Puebla governor who refused to publish the 14 August 1867 Convocatoria and was removed by Juárez using extraordinary powers.

## Creative Use

Use as a state-level Liberal figure showing restored-republican conflict between central constitutional reform policy and state authority.
