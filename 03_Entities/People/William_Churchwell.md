# William Churchwell

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.150 presents Churchwell as Buchanan's special envoy who formed an impression from Lerdo and Ocampo that concessions might follow recognition, while Hamnett cautions that they did not explicitly promise concessions.

## Creative Use

Use him as an intermediary whose report could exaggerate or translate Mexican ambiguity into U.S. expectations.
