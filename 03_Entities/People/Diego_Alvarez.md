# Diego Álvarez

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.181 presents Diego Álvarez as the Guerrero officeholder whom Juárez kept in place and later appointed Governor and Military Commander under a state of siege.

## Creative Use

Use as a local Guerrero ally whose continuation in office preserves republican control while destabilizing the Álvarez-Jiménez alliance.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.212-213 Guerrero Civil War Notes

Hamnett pp.212-213 makes Diego Álvarez central to the Guerrero conflict: he is evaluated against Juan Álvarez, holds Hacienda La Providencia with armed followers, refuses withdrawal, protests Arce's garrisons, and is later asked by Juárez to restore Arce.
