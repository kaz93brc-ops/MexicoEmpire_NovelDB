# Rafael Cravioto

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.208-209 identifies Rafael Cravioto of Huauchinango as one of the few supports available to Puebla governor Rafael J. García and as a monitor of sierra developments.

## Creative Use

Use as a local ally whose position shows the state government's thin reach into the Puebla sierra.

Hamnett pp.210-211 presents Cravioto as urging Juárez to secure Zacapoaxtla and Zacatlán, and as joining Alatorre in arguing that Puebla sierra operations required federal funding.
