# Juan José Baz

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.103 identifies Juan José Baz with the 1858 founding of the radical Jalisco newspaper La Bandera Roja. Hamnett pp.194-195 later presents Baz as Díaz's appointee as jefe político of the Federal District and as a defender of Maximilian's execution as the sacrifice of a political principle.

## Creative Use

Use as a marker of radical Liberal rhetoric that turns execution into a claim about destroying the principle represented by Maximilian.

## Open Questions

- Confirm founding date, place, Baz's office, and the paper's political program.
- Confirm the exact date of Díaz's appointment, Baz's office title, and the source of his execution-defense statement in Chapter 8 note 46.
