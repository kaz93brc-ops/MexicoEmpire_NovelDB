# Victor Hugo

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.194-195 lists Victor Hugo among European figures sympathetic to Juárez and the Mexican republican cause who nevertheless appealed for Maximilian's life.

## Creative Use

Use as a transatlantic republican voice whose clemency appeal complicates a simple pro-Juárez versus pro-Maximilian split.
