# Joaquín Mauleón

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.223 presents Joaquín Mauleón as a Juárez relative, Oaxaca state deputy, and informant who reported on juarista survival and the Díaz brothers' reconciliation, while also attaching a reliability caveat to him.

## Creative Use

Use as a compromised or uncertain family informant whose reports give Juárez partial visibility into Oaxaca politics.
