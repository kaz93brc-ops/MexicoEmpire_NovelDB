# Miguel Negrete

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.139 identifies Miguel Negrete as a close ally of Jesús González Ortega who became one of Juárez's irreconcilable enemies after the 1865 succession conflict.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.210-211 Notes

Hamnett pp.210-211 has Negrete appear in Puebla on 3 February 1869, issue anti-Juárez constitutional and electoral claims, fail to recruit Félix Díaz, lose to Alatorre at Tepeji, and escape with about 200 followers.

## Hamnett 1994 p.216 Cadena Notes

Hamnett p.216 links Negrete politically with Trinidad García de la Cadena from the mid-1860s until their 1886 defeat by Díaz, and connects both men to Zacatecas regionalism and support for urban and peasant movements.
