# French General Douay

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.217 states that French General Douay subsidized Manuel Lozada in exchange for 3000 men for the Imperial Army; the general's full name remains to be verified.

## Creative Use

Use as a French military intermediary showing how the Empire bargained with regional armed power rather than commanding a unified loyalist bloc.
