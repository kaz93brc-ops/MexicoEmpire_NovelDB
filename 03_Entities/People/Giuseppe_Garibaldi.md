# Giuseppe Garibaldi

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.89 says Pius IX viewed some Mexican Liberals by analogy with Italian nationalist figures such as Garibaldi. Hamnett pp.194-195 later lists Garibaldi among Juárez/republican sympathizers whose clemency appeal for Maximilian reached Mexico after the execution.

## Creative Use

Use as a marker of transatlantic republican symbolism that does not map neatly onto support for Maximilian's execution.
