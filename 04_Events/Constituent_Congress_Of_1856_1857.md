# Constituent Congress of 1856-1857

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.63 presents the 1856-57 Congress as the arena where a radical majority produced a constitution fundamentally incompatible with Comonfort's preferences.

## Creative Use

Use as a political arena where Liberal factionalism becomes institutional conflict over constitutional design.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the composition of the constitutional commission, the moderate bloc, and the sequence of Comonfort's proposed amendments.
