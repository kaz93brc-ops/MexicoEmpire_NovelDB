# Electoral law of 4 August 1864

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.206-207 says the itinerant republican government issued an electoral law on 1864-08-04 that treated clergy as citizens able to vote and hold public office.

## Creative Use

Use as a legal hinge for Juárez's postwar church modus vivendi without turning it into simple anti-clerical or pro-clerical policy.

## Open Questions
