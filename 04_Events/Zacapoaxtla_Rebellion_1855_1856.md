# Zacapoaxtla Rebellion, 1855-1856

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.97 describes the Zacapoaxtla rebellion as led by a parish priest, linked to the Puebla city attempt, and successful in taking Puebla city on 1856-01-23.

## Creative Use

Use it to keep local religious defense, centralist constitutional politics, and Conservative military mobilization together without reducing rebels to simple reaction.
