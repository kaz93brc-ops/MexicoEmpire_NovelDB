## Hamnett 1994 p.173 Miramar Burden Notes

Hamnett p.173 records the Treaty of Miramar's French troop and Foreign Legion commitments, the transfer of intervention costs and old debts to Mexican resources, and Hamnett's interpretation that the treaty became both Maximilian's burden and Napoleon III's escape clause.
