# Bazaine Anti Guerrilla Instruction Of 10 April 1864

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.182-183 reports that Bazaine instructed commanders to treat juarista bands as bandits subject to martial law regardless of members' military or administrative rank.

## Creative Use

Use as the earlier French command step before the harsher 1865 decree and circular.
