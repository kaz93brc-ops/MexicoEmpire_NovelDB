# Amnesty of Porfirio Diaz, 1872

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.234 says Porfirio Diaz reached Mexico City on 17 November 1872 and received amnesty from President Lerdo after Juarez's death removed the immediate La Noria pretext.

## Creative Use

Use the amnesty as a post-defeat political pause for Diaz, not as a direct line to the later Porfiriato.

## Open Questions

- Confirm how the amnesty related to the remaining La Noria networks.
