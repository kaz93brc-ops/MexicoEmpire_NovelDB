# Civil War In Guerrero

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.211 opens a new section under this title and frames Juárez as drawn into a Guerrero power struggle involving the Álvarez clientele and Vicente Jiménez supporters.

## Creative Use

Use as the entry point for tracking post-Intervention republican conflict outside Puebla, with caution until the next page completes the unfinished sentence.

## Open Questions
