# Plan Of Ayutla

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.57 treats the original Plan of Ayutla as a southern regional pronouncement against Santa Anna's centralizing dictatorship, conscription, and capitation tax, not yet as a full Liberal reform manifesto.

## Creative Use

Use to separate anti-Santa Anna mobilization from later Liberal reform ideology.

## Open Questions

- Compare the original plan text with the 1854-03-11 Acapulco revision.
