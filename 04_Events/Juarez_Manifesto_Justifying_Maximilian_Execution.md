# Juárez Manifesto justifying Maximilian's execution

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.194-195 summarizes this Manifesto as Juárez's public justification of his conduct after Maximilian's execution, using Anáhuac, anti-European-monarchy, anti-colonial, and republican-democratic language.
