# Lerdo Resignation 1860-05-30

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.123 says Juárez accepted Miguel Lerdo's resignation on 1860-05-30 after the cabinet refused to concur with Lerdo's recommendation to suspend payment of Mexico's foreign debt.

## Creative Use
