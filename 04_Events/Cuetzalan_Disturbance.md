# Cuetzalan Disturbance

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.209 links disturbance in Cuetzalan to restored Liberal efforts to reimpose ecclesiastical disamortization and to wider popular discontent in the Puebla sierra.

## Creative Use

Use as a local example where Church property policy, popular politics, and regional unrest intersect after the Empire's collapse.
