# Law of 25 June 1862

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.189 says the Juárez government invoked the law of 25 June 1862 when directing Escobedo to try Maximilian, Miramón, and Mejía by court martial.

## Creative Use

Use as the legal hinge between wartime sovereignty claims and the later trial, while keeping the law's actual text and application logic open for verification.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the statute text, penalties, jurisdiction, and the legal basis for applying it to the three prisoners.

## Hamnett 1994 pp.190-191 Defence and Government Arguments

Hamnett pp.190-191 records Escobedo rejecting Maximilian's inapplicability claim, the defence arguing the law did not fit a recognized three-year government or a reconciliation period, and Lerdo/Juárez maintaining the law's applicability.
