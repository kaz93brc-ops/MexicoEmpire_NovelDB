# Imperial Decree Of 3 October 1865

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.182-183 treats the decree as a coercive imperial measure against unauthorized armed groups and their supporters, while keeping its responsibility layered among Maximilian, Bazaine, and Conservative pressure.

## Creative Use

Use as the legal instrument that connects anti-guerrilla violence, Maximilian's responsibility problem, and the later court martial.
