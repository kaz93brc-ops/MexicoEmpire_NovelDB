# McLane-Ocampo Treaty

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.150-151 frames the treaty as a product of Washington's anxieties and the Juárez government's urgent need for support under the Veracruz threat; it was signed on 1859-12-14 but not ratified by either side.

## Creative Use

Use as a survival compromise rather than a simple act of betrayal or pure self-defense: territory, recognition, loans, transit, and intervention safeguards all need separate treatment.

## Open Questions

- Confirm treaty text, signature, ratification failure, Juárez's November 1860 rejection, and the exact transit and intervention clauses.
