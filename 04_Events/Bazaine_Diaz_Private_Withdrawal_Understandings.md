# Bazaine-Diaz Private Withdrawal Understandings

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.179 reports private understandings between Bazaine and other French commanders and Díaz and other Mexican generals not to attack French forces during evacuation.

## Creative Use

Use as a scene and strategy hinge showing how French withdrawal could isolate Maximilian's imperial forces while sparing retreating French columns.
