# Juárez Arrest 1857-12-17

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.82-83 states that Juárez was among the cabinet members arrested during the Plan of Tacubaya coup and interprets the arrest as evidence that Comonfort did not trust Juárez in the plot.

## Creative Use

Use as a high-pressure scene while separating the arrest as historical sequence from Hamnett's inference about Juárez's non-participation.
