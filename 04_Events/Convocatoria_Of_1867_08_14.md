# Convocatoria of 1867-08-14

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.202-203 presents Juárez's 14 August 1867 Convocatoria as a proposal to ask the electorate to approve constitutional reforms before full constitutional restoration.

## Creative Use

Use as a restored-republican turning point where war emergency authority, constitutional reform, and Liberal factional conflict converge.
