# Mathews Mediation Plan 1860

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.123-124 presents George Mathews's mediation plan as a diplomatic-corps proposal that Degollado authorized Mathews to publish and that implied removing Juárez as a peace obstacle.

## Creative Use

Use as an internal Liberal crisis in which foreign mediation, state governors, and a provisional presidency threatened Juárez's constitutional claim.
