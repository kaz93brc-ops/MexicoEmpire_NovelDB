# Battle of San Jacinto, 1867

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.186 identifies San Jacinto as the battle where Mariano Escobedo defeated Miguel Miramón on 1 February 1867, forcing Miramón back toward Querétaro.

## Creative Use

Use as the military reversal that turns Miramón's Zacatecas threat into an imperial retreat and introduces the harsh prisoner-execution aftermath.

- Confirm the precise location, battle course, and force sizes.
