# Colonization Law of 5 September 1865

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.158-159 presents Maximilian's colonization law as both formally anti-slavery for people entering Mexico and institutionally open to coercive labor contracts.

## Creative Use

Use to complicate Maximilian's modernization image by separating emancipatory language from labor-control mechanisms and Confederate-resettlement politics.
