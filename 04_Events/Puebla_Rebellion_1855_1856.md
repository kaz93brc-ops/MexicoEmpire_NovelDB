# Puebla Rebellion, 1855-1856

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.96-97 presents the Puebla rebellion as a serious blow to the Ayutla regime and to Comonfort's policy of conciliation after the Juárez Law.

## Creative Use

Use the event to show how an apparently jurisdictional reform produced episcopal protest, local rebellion, Conservative military mobilization, and government overreaction.
