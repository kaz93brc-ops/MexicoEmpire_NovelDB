# Plan Of Tacubaya

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.82 treats the Plan of Tacubaya coup as the outcome of Liberal division over constitutional reform, Comonfort's alliance with Zuloaga, and the regular army's leverage against Congress.

## Creative Use

Use as the constitutional crisis that turns Liberal factionalism and executive-legislative conflict into a military-backed presidential coup.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the plan text, proclamation date, signatories, and Comonfort-Zuloaga coordination.
- Distinguish Comonfort's intended constitutional revision from Zuloaga's later Conservative seizure of power.
