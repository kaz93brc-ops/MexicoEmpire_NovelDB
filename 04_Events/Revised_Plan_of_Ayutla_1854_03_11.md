# Revised Plan Of Ayutla 1854 03 11

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.57 identifies Comonfort's 1854-03-11 Acapulco revision as the stage at which the Plan of Ayutla took on explicit Liberal coloration and hinted at reviving Gomez Farias-era reforms.

## Creative Use

Use to show a shift from regional anti-dictatorial pronouncement to a broader Liberal reform coalition.
