# Manifesto to the Nation

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.105 states that Juárez, Ocampo, Ruiz, and Lerdo issued this manifesto on 1859-07-07 before the promulgation of the Reform Laws.

Hamnett p.106-107 presents the Manifesto as reaffirming civil authority over clergy, announcing Church-state separation, Civil Registry, religious freedom, property nationalization principles, and a compromise between Ocampo's social-base aims and Lerdo's fiscal disamortization aims.

## Creative Use

Use as a public ideological statement from the Veracruz Liberal government, while keeping Sierra's later evaluation separate from the document's text.
