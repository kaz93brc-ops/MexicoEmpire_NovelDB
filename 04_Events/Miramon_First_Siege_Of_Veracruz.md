# Miramón's First Siege of Veracruz

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.151 treats Miramón's June-July 1859 threat to Veracruz as a military pressure that made U.S. support especially urgent for the Juárez government during the recognition and treaty negotiations.

## Creative Use

Use this event to place diplomatic concessions inside a real military emergency rather than as detached treaty making.
