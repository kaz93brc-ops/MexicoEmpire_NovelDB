# Bazaine Confidential Circular Of 11 October 1865

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.183 presents Bazaine's confidential circular as the operational instruction that specified reprisals, immediate shooting of armed persons, no prisoner exchange, and terrorizing anti-juarista methods.

## Creative Use

Use as a private command document that turns the bandits-versus-belligerents logic into field practice.
