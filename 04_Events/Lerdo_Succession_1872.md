# Lerdo succession, 1872

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.234 says the news of Juarez's death went to Sebastian Lerdo de Tejada as constitutional successor and interprets Lerdo as inheriting the unresolved problems of the late Juarez administration.

## Creative Use

Use this as a procedural and political burden placed on Lerdo, not simply as personal advancement.
