# Pastoral Letter 1859-08-30

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.110 treats this Mexico City Pastoral Letter as an episcopal response to the Veracruz Reform Laws: it defended clergy, reaffirmed Church and Holy See authority, condemned the laws, and framed Juárez as hostile to the Catholic Church.

## Creative Use

Use as a document scene in which bishops answer Liberal accusations and turn Reform Law legality into a public struggle over authority, morality, and wartime blame.
