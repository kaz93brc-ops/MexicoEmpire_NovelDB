# Alleged Territorial Concession Scheme, October 1864

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.161 reports Frías y Soto's later claim that an October 1864 territorial-concession idea came from Romero and Doblado as a device to attract Washington support, not from Juárez as a settled policy.

## Creative Use
