## Hamnett 1994 p.96-97 Connection

Hamnett pp.96-97 treats the law as an administrative justice law issued under Alvarez's executive authority before congress, aimed at limiting exemptions from civil law without immediately abolishing corporate privilege. The page also records episcopal protests and the Puebla/Zacapoaxtla crisis that followed.
