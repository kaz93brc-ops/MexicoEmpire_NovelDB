# Juárez Cabinet Remodeling 1861

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.55 reports Guzmán's view that the 1861 cabinet remodeling still preserved a progressive color through Zarco, Ramírez, Prieto, and others.

## Creative Use

## Hamnett 1994 p.127 Cabinet Conflict Notes

Hamnett p.127 presents González Ortega and Doblado as pressing against radicals in Juárez's cabinet while Hamnett characterizes the 1861 cabinet as Juárez's most radical, naming Zarco, Ignacio Ramírez, and Prieto with portfolios.
