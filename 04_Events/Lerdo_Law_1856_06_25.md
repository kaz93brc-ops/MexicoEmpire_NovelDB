# Lerdo Law 1856-06-25

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.65 presents Miguel Lerdo's June 1856 law as a measure for converting corporate property into individual ownership, with implications for Church property and Indigenous communal land.

## Creative Use

Use as the moment Liberal Reform enters rural society and landholding relationships in a more contentious way.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the official title, text, promulgation date, and congressional support.
- Confirm the law's scope for Church property and Indigenous communal land.
- Check note 22 and later Chapter 4 discussion.
