## Hamnett 1994 p.72-73 Notes

Hamnett p.72-73 summarizes the 1857 Constitution as a federal constitution with a weak national executive, strong legislature, unicameral Congress, no presidential veto, incorporated Juárez Law and Lerdo Law, clergy exclusion, rights and individual guarantees, popular sovereignty, state autonomy under federal constitutional supremacy, and separation of powers. He also stresses internal centralism within states through governors and jefes políticos, and presents Emilio Rabasa's later criticism as a separate historiographical voice.
