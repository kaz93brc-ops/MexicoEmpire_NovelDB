# Puebla Under Siege, 1872

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.210-211 says Juárez placed Puebla under siege on 5 March 1872 and removed Ignacio Romero Vargas's civil authority amid continuing La Noria-era turmoil.

## Creative Use

Use as a scene point for the tension between Juárez's civil-constitutional ideals and military emergency rule in a rebellious state.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the formal legal basis, decree wording, scope of the siege measure, and Alatorre's subsequent authority.
