# Reform War

## Summary

1858-61年の内戦。Hamnettは1847年敗北とFrench Interventionの間に、政治的遠心力を再開した危機として位置づける。

## Hamnett 1994 p.120-121 Notes

Hamnett pp.120-121 adds three Reform War dimensions: Liberal military disadvantage was partly offset by port and border revenue access, that revenue remained decentralized under governors and regional powers, and the Veracruz government faced internal Liberal tensions around Gutiérrez Zamora, Miguel Lerdo, Ocampo, and Ruiz.

## Hamnett 1994 p.122-123 Notes

Hamnett pp.122-123 adds internal Liberal crises to the Reform War: Ocampo-Degollado correspondence publication, Vidaurri's repudiation, command transfer to Doblado, Lerdo's mediation and resignation, and Degollado's later contact with George Mathews.
