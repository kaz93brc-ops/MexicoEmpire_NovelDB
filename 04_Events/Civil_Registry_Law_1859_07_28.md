# Civil Registry Law 1859-07-28

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.109 states that a Civil Registry system was established in Liberal-held territory on 1859-07-28, and notes Juárez's symbolic use of the Veracruz Registry for his newborn son.

## Creative Use

Use as the administrative transfer of birth, marriage, and death registration from ecclesiastical monopoly toward state citizenship records.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the official legal title, Spanish text, and scope in Liberal-held territory.
- Confirm the name of Juárez's newborn son registered at Veracruz.
- Clarify how this 1859 system related to the 1857 Lafragua Law and the 1859 Manifesto.
