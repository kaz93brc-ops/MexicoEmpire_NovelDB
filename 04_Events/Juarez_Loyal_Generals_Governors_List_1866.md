# Juárez's Loyal Generals and Governors List, July 1866

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.181 reports that in July 1866 Juárez made a list of generals and governors whose loyalty he expected, though several names remain surname-only in this capture.

## Creative Use

Use as an index of Juárez's wartime trust calculus, not as proof of unquestioned loyalty.

## Open Questions

- Confirm Chapter 8 note 22 and the list's original document.
