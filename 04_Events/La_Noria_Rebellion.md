# La Noria rebellion

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.200-201 lists the 1871-72 La Noria rebellion among the challenges Juárez survived during the late Juárez period.

## Creative Use

Use as a late-restored-republic crisis in which Liberal military opposition to Juárez's re-election became open revolt.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the Plan of La Noria date, text, signatories, and regional spread.
- Confirm Porfirio Díaz's role and how Hamnett sources the rebellion in Chapter 9 notes.
- Confirm how the rebellion relates to the 1871 re-election conflict.
