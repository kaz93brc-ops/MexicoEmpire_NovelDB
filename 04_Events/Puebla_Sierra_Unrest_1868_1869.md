# Puebla Sierra Unrest, 1868-1869

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.208-209 presents the Puebla sierra as a dangerous post-Intervention conflict zone where state government, local chieftains, military officers, and Juárez's central authority collided.

## Creative Use

Use as a setting for republican internal conflict over legality, provincial autonomy, military resources, and local legitimacy.

## Open Questions

- Confirm exact dates, leaders, towns, and whether the 1869 military-led rebellion should be split from 1868 sierra negotiations.
