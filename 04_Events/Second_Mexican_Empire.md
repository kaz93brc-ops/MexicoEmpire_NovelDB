## Hamnett 1994 pp.172-173 Establishment and Limits Notes

Hamnett pp.172-173 treats the Empire's establishment as a coalition mismatch: Conservative hopes for Reform rollback clashed with French command policy, Maximilian sought constitutional and national-consensus legitimacy, and Miramar plus French military dependence created structural weakness.
