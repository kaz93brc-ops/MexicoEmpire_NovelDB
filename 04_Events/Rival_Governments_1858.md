# Rival Governments 1858

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.83 frames early 1858 as the point when Mexico had two opposed governments, beginning the three-year Reform War.

## Creative Use

Use as the structural setup for civil war: constitutional succession outside Mexico City against a Conservative regime in the capital.

## Open Questions
