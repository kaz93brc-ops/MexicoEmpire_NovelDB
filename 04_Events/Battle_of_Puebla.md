## Hamnett 1994 pp.154-155 Diplomatic Respite Notes

Hamnett pp.154-155 uses the 5 May 1862 French defeat at Puebla as a strategic pause: intervention forces were pushed back toward the Gulf hinterland, their advance halted for about a year, and Juárez's government gained time to seek arms in the United States.

## Hamnett 1994 p.171 Strategic Delay Notes

Hamnett p.171 treats the 5 May 1862 French defeat at Puebla as an early military failure that exposed political and military weaknesses in Napoleon III's design and delayed the French advance and imperial establishment for about a year.
