# Ecclesiastical Property Nationalization Law 1859-07-12

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.107 presents the 1859 Veracruz law on ecclesiastical property nationalization and Church-state separation as going far beyond the 1856 Lerdo Law by putting Church property up for public sale and directing sale revenue to the national treasury.

## Creative Use

Use as the institutional moment where Liberal wartime finance, public debt, corporate privilege, property law, and religious liberty converge in a coercive executive measure.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the official Spanish title, promulgation text, and exact date.
- Confirm whether the law's title combines nationalization and separation or whether Hamnett is describing two related laws.
- Confirm the cash/bond ratio, forty-month payment rule, and sanctions for purchasers returning property to the Church.
