## Hamnett 1994 p.28-29 Continuation

p.28 continues the p.27 discourse material. Hamnett presents the discourse as linking Hidalgo memory with republican government, liberty, reason, rights, and Mexico as a republic of free people, while also noting that the surviving evidence does not show the discourse's immediate political effect.
