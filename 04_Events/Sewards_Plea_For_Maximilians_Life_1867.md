# Seward's Plea for Maximilian's Life, 1867

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.162 treats Seward's request for Maximilian's life as provoking republican criticism, especially from Iglesias, who saw the State Department posture as an arrogant demand.

## Creative Use

Use as a diplomatic pressure point where humanitarian language and Mexican sovereignty claims collide.
