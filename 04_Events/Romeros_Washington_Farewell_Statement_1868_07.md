# Romero's Washington Farewell Statement, July 1868

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.162-163 reports Romero's July 1868 Washington departure statement as praising the United States as the first great power to treat Mexico as an equal, while Hamnett qualifies that view. p.165 note 33 identifies BJDOCS vol. 13, pp.411-12 as the source trail.

## Creative Use
