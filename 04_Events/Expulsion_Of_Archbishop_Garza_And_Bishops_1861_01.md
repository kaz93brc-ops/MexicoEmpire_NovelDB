# Expulsion Of Archbishop Garza And Bishops 1861-01

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.112 says Archbishop Garza and five fellow bishops were ordered to leave Mexico five days after the Apostolic Delegate was expelled; the 1861-01-17 date is inferred from that sequence.
