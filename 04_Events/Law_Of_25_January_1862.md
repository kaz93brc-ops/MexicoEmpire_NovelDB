# Law Of 25 January 1862

## Summary

Hamnett p.175 states that the 25 January 1862 law put the allied intervention forces outside the law and prescribed death for collaboration.

## Creative Use

Use to show the severity of republican wartime law while separating sovereignty defense from later moral simplification.
