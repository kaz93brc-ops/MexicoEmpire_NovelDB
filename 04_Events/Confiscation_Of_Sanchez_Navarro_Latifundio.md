# Confiscation Of Sanchez Navarro Latifundio

## Historical Role

Hamnett pp.176-177 presents the confiscation of the Sánchez Navarro latifundio as a political punishment for imperial collaboration that began in earnest with a state governor's decree of 22 November 1865 and continued in 1866.

## Creative Use

Use as a concrete case where republican law, local authority, landholding, and wartime collaboration converge.

## Open Questions
