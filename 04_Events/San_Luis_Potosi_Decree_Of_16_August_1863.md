# San Luis Potosi Decree Of 16 August 1863

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.176 presents this San Luis Potosí decree as defining officials who served the intervention-backed power, and those who defended them, as traitors subject to graduated penalties.

## Creative Use

Use as a legal hinge between emergency resistance and later punishment of imperial collaboration.
