# Imperial Decrees On Bienes Nacionales 1864-1865

## Summary

Hamnett p.174 presents the imperial decrees of 28 December 1864 and 26 February 1865 as confirming purchasers of bienes nacionales and reopening Church-property sales under conditions.

## Creative Use

Use as the policy moment in which Maximilian disappoints clerical restoration hopes while protecting property purchasers.
