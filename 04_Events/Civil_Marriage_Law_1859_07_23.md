# Civil Marriage Law 1859-07-23

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.108 presents the 1859-07-23 civil marriage law as a Veracruz Reform measure that made marriage a civil contract before state authority while leaving religious blessing to each confession's rules.

## Creative Use

Use as the family-law point where Church-state separation reaches household status, sacramental access, civil sovereignty, and the limits of nineteenth-century divorce.

## Open Questions

- Confirm the official Spanish title, promulgation text, and relationship to the broader Veracruz Reform Laws.
- Confirm Ruiz's explanation and Juárez's Doblado letter through notes 35 and 36.
- Do not confuse the law's separation mechanism with later remarriage-permitting divorce.
