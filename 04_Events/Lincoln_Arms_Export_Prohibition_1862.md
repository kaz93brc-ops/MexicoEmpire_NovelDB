# Lincoln Arms Export Prohibition 1862

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.156 dates Lincoln's arms-export prohibition to 2 November 1862 and connects it to reduced republican resistance capacity when French military pressure resumed.

## Creative Use

Use as a policy-level reversal of Juárez's post-Puebla arms-procurement hopes, not as proof that Lincoln supported the French project.
