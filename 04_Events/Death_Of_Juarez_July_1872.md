# Death of Juarez, July 1872

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.234 places Juarez's death at 11:30 p.m. on 18 July 1872 after repeated heart attacks earlier that year and treats the death as a turning point in the La Noria aftermath.

## Creative Use

Use the event as a constitutional transfer and unfinished-reform moment rather than a simple closing triumph.
