# Estatuto Organico Provisional De La Republica

## Historical Role

Hamnett p.64 treats this Comonfort government measure as an attempt to give legal form to the provisional regime before the new constitution.

## Creative Use

Use as the legal flashpoint through which Oaxaca Liberals challenge Comonfort's centralizing tendencies.
