## Summary

p.84 completes the previous page's unfinished sentence. Hamnett closes Chapter 4 by arguing that the Conservative military overthrow of the constitutional system did not directly help Juárez, but it gave Liberal defense of the 1857 Constitution a moral and institutional justification. Hamnett also says Juárez's career makes his support for constitutional government in Mexico hard to doubt, while the 1857 Constitution itself later became a political burden for him.
