## Summary

This spread continues Chapter 5 notes from note 7 through note 42. Notes 7-16 form a background source trail for conservative thought, Juárez studies, Oaxaca state politics, Church history, and Liberalism, including secondary literature, BJDOCS, and AGEO FJ references.

Notes 17-22 point to Puebla rebellion, 1856-57 politics, Miramón, Mexican Liberal thought, central Mexican peasant society, and Catholic-side argument. Note 22 is important because it identifies J. J. Pesado in La Cruz as a source locator for Catholic position material.

Notes 23-34 collect source trails for Juárez correspondence, BJDOCS, Taracena, Bulnes, Sierra, Carmen Blázquez, Miguel Lerdo de Tejada, the Lerdo Law, the Ayutla/Reform political process, and the Reform War.

Notes 35-36 identify dated document references from 1859-07-23 and a Juárez to Doblado letter from Veracruz on 1859-08-18. These strengthen the existing Veracruz Reform Laws timelines rather than requiring new timeline entries.

Notes 37-42 provide the key follow-up for the previous capture: Knowlton on Church property, Staples on state and Church, Galindo y Galindo across the Reform War to Maximilian, Bastien and Baldwin on Protestantism, BJDOCS volume 4 for January 1861 Church measures and Zarco, and BJDOCS/Knowlton for Church property and public debt.
