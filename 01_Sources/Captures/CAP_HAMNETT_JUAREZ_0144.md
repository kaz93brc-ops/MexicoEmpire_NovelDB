## Summary

- p.282 is Map 1, *Mexico, 1821-53*. It distinguishes the northern territory lost to the United States in 1848 from the narrower 1853 Gadsden Purchase strip and is a political-geography aid, not a Maximilian-era campaign map.
- p.283 is Map 2, *The State of Oaxaca, 1857*. It presents Oaxaca as a composite space of central valley, northern highlands, western Mixteca zones, south-western coast, and eastern Isthmus rather than a single undifferentiated Juárez base.

## Map 1 Relative Geography

- Upper California is shown as the large north-western Pacific territory north of Lower California and within the 1848 loss zone; no new Place stub was created from this map alone.
- [[Lower_California|Lower California]] is the peninsula west of [[Sonora]], contrasted with Upper California and retained on the Mexican side.
- [[Sonora]] and [[Chihuahua]] lie immediately south of the 1848 loss zone and form the northern edge of the remaining Mexican territory.
- [[New_Mexico|New Mexico]] lies north of Sonora / Chihuahua, west of [[Texas]], and east of Upper California within the lost inland territory.
- Texas lies east of New Mexico and north-east of Chihuahua, opening toward the Gulf of Mexico.
- The Gadsden Purchase is separately hatched as a narrow 1853 strip cutting the northern margins of Sonora and Chihuahua. This connects to the Mesilla Treaty memory in [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_1796|Fact1796]] and [[FACT_HAMNETT_JUAREZ_1947|Fact1947]], but no new Event or Timeline was created.
- Use with [[Mexican_American_War|Mexican-American War]], [[Loss_of_Texas_1836|loss of Texas]], [[Territorial_Cession_And_Transit_Rights|territorial cession and transit rights]], [[United_States_Pressure|United States pressure]], and sovereignty themes; do not infer detailed legal boundaries from the map alone.

## Map 2 Relative Geography

- [[Oaxaca]] is read as several regional zones: [[Oaxaca_Central_Valley|central valley]], [[Northern_Sierra_Oaxaca|Northern Highlands]], [[Mixteca_Baja|Mixteca Baja]], [[Mixteca_Alta|Mixteca Alta]], Coastal Mixteca, and [[Isthmus_Of_Tehuantepec|Isthmus]]. Coastal Mixteca remains a map label rather than a new Place card.
- [[Oaxaca_City|Oaxaca City]] is the central administrative anchor. The map is used to show its distance from surrounding zones, not to repeat a list of major cities.
- Northern Highlands lies north to north-east of the capital and includes [[Guelatao]], [[Ixtlan|Ixtlán]], and [[Ixtepeji]]. Guelatao is Juárez's mountain birthplace north-east of the capital, not an immediate suburb; Ixtlán lies eastward of Guelatao, while Ixtepeji sits on the more southerly approach from Oaxaca City. The screenshot supports the spelling `Ixtepeji`, though the small label remains verification-needed.
- Mixteca Baja lies in north-west Oaxaca around [[Huajuapan]], with Tehuacán outside the state as a north-western connection. Mixteca Alta lies farther west to south-west inland toward [[Tlaxiaco]] and Juxtlahuaca.
- The west / north-west approach is summarized as Tlapa → Silacayoapan → Juxtlahuaca → Tlaxiaco → Teposcolula → Nochixtlán → Oaxaca; Huajuapan lies north of this chain. These minor names are retained as relative-position notes, not exhaustively carded.
- South of Oaxaca City, Ocotlán and then Ejutla form an inland axis descending from the central valley.
- Coastal Mixteca occupies the south-west Pacific coast around Jamiltepec. Ometepec lies to its west outside Oaxaca; [[Pochutla]] lies east of Jamiltepec and west of Tehuantepec as a coastal intermediate point.
- The Isthmus occupies eastern to south-eastern Oaxaca. [[Tehuantepec]] lies toward the Pacific side; [[Juchitan|Juchitán]] is nearby but farther east and closer to the Chiapas side. Both are distant from Oaxaca City and belong to a distinct regional-political setting.
- R. Coatzacoalcos is shown north of the Isthmus flowing toward the Gulf of Mexico and helps explain the trans-isthmian connection. It remains a locator candidate under the Isthmus theme rather than a new river Place.
- Orizaba and Córdoba are external Veracruz-side anchors north to north-east of Oaxaca and remain contextual labels only.
