# Mata discussed a two million dollar loan with Buchanan in July 1858

## Claim

In early July 1858, Mata discussed a two million dollar loan with Buchanan, including issues of the Isthmus, a projected railway from West Texas through Chihuahua and Sonora to the Gulf of California, and free ports at both ends of the route.
