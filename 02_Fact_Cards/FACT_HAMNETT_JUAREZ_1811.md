# The Juárez government conceded Tehuantepec and northern-Pacific transit routes

## Claim

With Miramón's threat to Veracruz growing, the Juárez government conceded transit across the Isthmus of Tehuantepec and from Arizona and Texas to Guaymas and Mazatlán.
